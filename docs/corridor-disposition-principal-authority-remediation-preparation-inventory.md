## Required authority separation

1. Operator Root decides whether one exact successor generation may add only corridor-disposition
   scope. The grant cannot originate from the current principal, MasterMason, a lifecycle record,
   an eligibility result or the corridor producer.
2. MasterMason may mechanically consume that exact grant into one immutable successor initially
   held at `PENDING_ACTIVATION`. It cannot choose scope or activate the successor.
3. A separate Operator Root lifecycle authority may activate that exact successor after canonical
   reconstruction proves lineage and single-winner succession.
4. Only the resulting effectively `ACTIVE` generation may originate one exact corridor caller-
   authority issuance decision. Issuance cannot select or seal the disposition.
5. Reconsideration Batch 5 may resume only after read-only reconstruction proves all four facts.

## Non-authorities

The current principal cannot widen itself. Constitution authority cannot be replayed as a scope
grant. `SUPERSEDE` cannot be reinterpreted to evade `authority_scope_changed=false`. MasterMason is
not the scope owner. A test fixture, target, dossier, eligibility record, reconstruction result,
custody refusal, activation artifact or offline demonstration creates no authority. Caller authority
cannot issue or consume itself, and neither candidate outcome can select itself.

## Smallest safe continuation sequence

No step is authorized merely because it appears here.

1. **Batch 1 — authority-empty contracts.** Define separate scope-grant, scope-successor and exact
   caller-authority-issuance contracts, including non-authorities and the return gate.
2. **Batch 2 — validators and immutable fixture stores.** Validate caller-supplied fixtures without
   identifying a live operator, principal or target and without issuing authority.
3. **Batch 3 — offline replay and interruption proof.** Prove grant issuance/consumption, successor
   commit, activation separation and caller issuance under expiry, revocation and contention.
4. **Batch 4 — read-only aggregate reconstruction.** Classify the complete chain as eligible,
   incomplete, conflicted or refused without creating authority.
5. **Batch 5 — separately authorized scope remediation producer.** Only with exact Operator Root
   authority, commit and separately activate one successor generation; then issue one exact corridor
   caller authority under a further explicit authorization.
6. **Terminal audit and return.** Prove current generation uniqueness, authority custody, no scope
   beyond corridor disposition, no artifact mutation and the continuing custody refusal. Only then
   may Provider Binding Activation Corridor Disposition Reconsideration Batch 5 resume.
