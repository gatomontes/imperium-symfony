# Next campaign: Provider Binding Successor Atomic Live Transition

## Selection

Provider Binding Successor Atomic Live Transition is separately selected after
Provider Binding Successor Live Adoption closed at
`PROVIDER_BINDING_SUCCESSOR_LIVE_ADOPTION_CAMPAIGN_COMPLETE_PRE_LIVE_TRANSITION_ONLY`.

Only Provider Binding Successor Atomic Live Transition Preparation Batch 0 is
authorized.

The completed readiness chain is prerequisite evidence. It is not runtime
transition authority. The provider binding remains BOUND_INACTIVE and v3
execution admission remains NOT_IMPLEMENTED.

## Preparation Batch 0 inventory

Preparation Batch 0 must inventory and classify:

- the exact runtime entry point, competent transition decision owner and
  executor principal;
- production of the exact live-transition decision and immutable provenance;
- single-use live-transition authority issuance, durable custody and
  process-local delivery;
- the existing combined same-root winner boundary and every validator required
  before mutation;
- the exact BOUND_INACTIVE source binding and successor target state;
- the persistence roots, lock scope, lock order and transaction primitive for
  authority consumption, v3 admission, successor adoption and binding
  transition as one atomic commit;
- the first irreversible write and every crash cut before and after it;
- exact replay, changed evidence, same-root contention, expiry and revocation;
- partial-write refusal, deterministic recovery and read-only reconstruction;
- the durable transition receipt, audit lineage and proof digest;
- recursive credential, secret and process-local capability exclusion;
- the final closed boundary before credential resolution, provider invocation,
  external I/O and provider effect start;
- threat-model assumptions, candidate boundary postures, refusal conditions and
  deferred boundaries.

Every finding must be classified as `EXISTS_CANONICALLY`,
`EXISTS_FRAGMENTED`, `ABSENT` or `DEFERRED_BOUNDARY`.

## Batch estimate

The planning estimate is nine batches including Preparation Batch 0:

0. executable-transition inventory and classification;
1. competent transition-decision producer and exact principal;
2. single-use transition-authority issuance, custody and delivery;
3. atomic persistence, lock and combined-winner contracts;
4. executable atomic consumption, v3 admission, adoption and binding transition;
5. interruption, replay, contention, expiry, revocation and recovery proof;
6. durable receipt and read-only reconstruction;
7. adversarial audit and secret-exclusion proof;
8. terminal audit and campaign closure.

A refusal or correction batch expands this estimate. No batch may infer
authority from a later planned batch.

or change runtime behavior.

They may not invoke a provider.
They may not perform external I/O.
They may not start a provider effect.
They may not authorize retry.

They may not open Iron Gate or Lazaretto.
