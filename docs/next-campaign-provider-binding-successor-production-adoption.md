# Next campaign: Provider Binding Successor Production Adoption

## Selection

Provider Binding Successor Production Adoption is separately selected after the
pre-provider reconciliation campaign closed. Begin Provider Binding Successor
Production Adoption Preparation Batch 0 only.

The campaign must determine the lawful production route for the reconciled,
operation-scoped provider-binding lifecycle successor. Selection does not make
the offline successor production evidence and does not connect it to the live
execution corridor.

## Preparation boundary

Preparation Batch 0 may inventory and classify:

- the exact competent decision owner and executor principal;
- decision issuance and single-use successor-creation authority;
- authority custody, atomic consumption and immutable successor creation;
- effect-start ordering and the existing combined-admission seam;
- crash recovery, replay, contention, expiry, revocation and reconstruction;
- live-consumer migration and adoption gaps;
- secret exclusion, threat-model alignment and non-authorities.

It may not define a production runtime contract or change runtime behavior. It
may not activate a provider binding. It may not issue or consume authority. It
may not handle or resolve a credential or capability. It may not invoke a
provider. It may not perform external I/O. It may not migrate a live command.
Iron Gate and Lazaretto remain closed.
