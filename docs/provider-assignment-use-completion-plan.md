## 1. Establish the reachable chronology first

Produce an explicit dependency graph from real source owners before adding a positive fixture: initial public-source admission and policy signing, base selection/mapping, FRESH founding, R4 establishment, native Profile model authorization/sealing/correspondence, designation and independent appointment, mapping preparation and admission, W1/W2/W3 assessment custody, application and delivery. Record when each exact artifact's bytes and schema-qualified reference become known, who may publish/admit it and which immutable earlier record references it.

`FreshInstitutionalProfileMapping::prepareInOwner` requires completed R4, the current native designation and its independent appointed holder, then emits an unadmitted source record. `AssignmentRule::tuples` requires that exact Profile reference to equal the policy target. `AssignmentRule::sets` loads the policy's fixed permitted tuple sets. `ApplicationOwner::replace` stays within the original policy/assessment view and a separately authorized exact set. These are concrete joins to prove. Test whether the actual earlier policy and source admission can reference the later produced originals without altered bytes, a second policy on a consumed root or an unsupported new admission authority. Do not assume a prepared mapping is admitted or a precomputed hash establishes provenance.

Keep initial application, binding-only replacement and Profile/holder-generation replacement distinct. An exact mapping embeds the Profile and holder generation: a fixture cannot change those under a retained reference. Prove the replacements the original policy actually permits. If a required replacement or initial sequence needs a new deferred-reference, policy amendment or source-admission contract, deliver the exact minimal incompatibility and reviewable proposed contract separately; do not silently implement new competence or weaken frozen O4 rules. Complete any independently reachable approved work and report partial scope. R4 does not reopen merely because R3 has a new join to resolve.

## 2. Substantive predicates and competent originals

Enumerate every policy-required Profile predicate and every selected role/binding combination. Produce a closed table: predicate ID and precise meaning, competent institutional producer, signed original/schema/identity, existing qualification/approval dependencies, derivation algorithm, currentness and expiry/revocation, limits, reached refusal and positive test. Trace the original requirements and actual policy rather than deleting or renaming requirements to fit the current verifier.

`NativeAssignmentEvidence` currently recognizes only `profile.current_active`, `profile.exact_model_binding` and `profile.independent_appointment`; it refuses unknown extras including historical `profile.fits`. `AssignmentRule::profileEvidence` only structurally checks admitted rows and declared PASS. `CandidateClaim::PREDICATES` are parsed cognition claims, not institutional facts. Preserve that separation. Add narrowly scoped producer/verifier mechanics only where the existing approved competence and retained originals establish their meaning. A generic owner-signed PASS, arbitrary evidence reference, narration or successful admission cannot create fitness authority. Do not define `profile.fits` as the conjunction of those three lifecycle facts without an existing substantive contract supporting that equivalence.

Resolve complete own Senate findings/reconciliation/security disposition, exact Profile approval, qualification results, native source/model authorization and immutable binding/configuration correspondence as applicable. Retain exact source identity, full artifact digest and all independent generations. Evidence from a predecessor Profile or appointment is not evidence for its successor. Bound incoming records, retained references, depth, total reconstruction bytes/work and history before publication/current validation. Unknown or unavailable substantive predicates must continue to refuse. External model capability/access/cognition facts stay separately missing or explicitly synthetic; no institutional signature substitutes for their production evidence.
