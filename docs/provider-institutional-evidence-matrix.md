## Concrete frozen-contract incompatibility

`contracts/profile-artifact.md`, lifecycle and installability, requires a competent destination steward's `current_active` designation after approval, at most one current version for steward/target, and a `SUPERSEDES` relation plus supersession attestation. An approved immutable artifact is insufficient. No synchronized designation/supersession owner for the O4 role/model/Profile tuple was found in the traced native paths.

`FormationPersonnel::candidate` authenticates the four Senate findings, competent approval and qualification; its generated lifecycle ends at `approved`. `FormationOfficerAssemblyService` returns `ASSEMBLED_FOR_EXACT_APPOINTMENT` with activation false. `FormationProfileContract` accepts a closed officer envelope that does not include a `model_binding` member. Changing those historical bytes or reinterpreting appointment generation as a new model-binding designation would weaken existing provenance, not supply the missing fact. Existing `PersistentSettings::verifyFormation` correctly joins an admitted mapping to the exact current holder; that consumer mapping does not itself create designation.

The actual ingress is also closed: `FormationPersonnel::record` maps the delegated `laboratorium` role only to `DERIVED_PROFILE`. A different designation kind fails `CMF041_PERSONNEL_EVIDENCE_INVALID`, even with that delegate's correct signature; putting a designation claim inside derivation content would not change the admitted competence. A narrow versioned designation/supersession kind and its consumer semantics therefore need review, rather than silently treating the existing derivation kind as a second effect. This is a source-level incompatibility witness; the executable standing-effect witness below addresses a different, Augur-specific boundary.

The standing Augur approval/designation/qualification effects are also explicitly rejected by `AuthorityAdmission\Rules::UNSUPPORTED`; `InstitutionalAssignmentContractTest` demonstrates actual correctly signed admission refusal without changing the journal. These are Augur-specific effects, **not** substitutes for Courtthane/Locksmith competence. Removing that refusal alone would not solve O4 current designation.

The alternative native institutional reader accepts only original `OPERATOR_ROOT_INSTALLATION` provenance and refuses governed successors (`CMF123_GOVERNED_SUCCESSOR_ADAPTER_REQUIRED`). Its incumbent files are outside the Formation journal's synchronized state. Importing an arbitrary snapshot through an H source proves neither later currentness nor atomic competing supersession. The existing assignment fixture deliberately uses a separate synthetic parent institution; that is test infrastructure, not a production designation source. Populating the FRESH root's native installation tree also conflicts with `OperatorRootOwnership::vacant` and the later `B225_FRESH_ROOT_OWNED` fence.

## Smallest proposed amendment, for review only

Approve a narrowly versioned owner bridge for the two O4 targets, preserving historical Formation envelopes and personnel semantics. Specify (1) the exact existing charter-assigned destination steward and authentication of its current tenure; (2) the governed approval, examination and qualification originals and their exact Profile version/content and model/configuration tuple; (3) a designation record, current Profile and binding generations, exact predecessor plus `SUPERSEDES`/revocation/expiry transitions; and (4) atomic publication and verification in the same Formation journal frame used by application and actual settings use. Define how external incumbent changes enter that synchronized owner boundary without treating a copied receipt as current tenure.

This requires an approved typed owner-state/schema integration, the narrow designation/supersession ingress kind, and source/admission semantics for those facts, not a new general trust domain, a new operator policy shortcut, or standing-personnel migration. The existing four-argument `AssignmentEvidence::verify` may receive an internal owner-frame integration analogous to `OwnerConstitutionEvidence`, without changing public requests or acquiring another lock. The bridge must explicitly distinguish approval from designation and settings selection from personnel appointment. The amendment and its implementation are **not performed here**.
