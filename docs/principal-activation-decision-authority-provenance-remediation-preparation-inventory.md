## Competent authority chain

The smallest lawful chain is:

1. Operator Root grants one narrow, expiring, single-use scope-remediation
   authority for the exact current Imperator generation.
2. A mechanical successor service consumes it into one immutable next-generation
   principal version held at PENDING_ACTIVATION.
3. A separately consumed lifecycle authority activates that successor and
   supersedes the prior generation without mutating either version.
4. The canonical caller-authority issuer reconstructs the successor as
   effectively ACTIVE and may issue only the exact decision and issuance
   transitions.
5. A canonical Imperator decision producer consumes the exact caller authority
   into one immutable decision and its single-use activation authority.
6. The Batch 1 activation service must resolve that canonical decision and
   source consumption rather than trust a caller-supplied array.
7. A read-only aggregate reconstruction proves the chain from Operator Root to
   the combined activation winner.

No step may issue its own prerequisite or create continuing authority.

## Ordering and contention

| Order | Winner identity | Required refusal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scope grant | Current generation + exact new scope + successor target | Wrong root, generation, scope, expiry, reuse or competing target |
| Successor commit | Prior generation + grant consumption | Missing prior version, non-current lifecycle, changed lineage or duplicate successor |
| Successor activation | Successor generation + lifecycle transition | Missing separate authority, expiry, revocation or competing disposition |
| Caller authority | Active generation + exact transition + exact target | Stale, suspended, superseded, revoked, expired or out-of-scope principal |
| Decision | Caller authority + attestation + assurance + boundary | Target mismatch, changed decision, contention or prior consumption |
| Principal activation | Exact canonical decision + generation target | Caller-supplied substitution, corruption, expiry, revocation or replay change |

Each transition must commit its consumption and result as one immutable winner
or prove a recoverable consume-to-commit relation. Activation, revocation and
supersession for the same lifecycle target must be mutually exclusive.

## Crash, replay and reconstruction

Before any combined commit, neither consumption nor result may exist. After
commit, exact replay must converge and changed evidence must conflict. Recovery
must reconstruct forward from immutable winners without reissuing scope,
successor, lifecycle authority, caller authority, decision or activation.

A process restart recreates no process-local capability identity. Corrupt or
conflicted evidence refuses without repair. Expired, revoked, suspended,
superseded or retired principals cannot issue or consume downstream authority.

UNKNOWN_REPLAY_PROHIBITED remains binding after any possible provider effect.

digest validity, filesystem possession, credential possession and same-process
location remain non-authorities unless the exact transition contract says
