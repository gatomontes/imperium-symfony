- `PREPARED` may prove journal validation and `REFUSE_AUTOMATIC_REPAIR` only.

  `REFUSE_PARTIAL_STATE` only.
- `COMMITTED` may prove exact journal/winner/receipt validation and
  `ACCEPT_EXACT_READ_ONLY` only. Replay or contention additionally requires a
  second complete evidence set.
- `INCOMPLETE` may prove only `REFUSE_INCOMPLETE_EVIDENCE` for the exact missing
  or malformed shape supplied.

No single classification proves the entire adversarial matrix.

## Digest and seal order

The smallest acyclic read-only evidence order is:

1. canonical fixture records;
2. typed mutation or unmodified-case input;
3. sealed adversarial case with expected deterministic result;
4. executed case result bound to input and output digests;
5. ordered case-result-set digest;
6. sealed read-only audit receipt bound to the complete required case set; and
7. terminal recomputation disposition bound to the audit-receipt digest.

No later digest may be placed into an earlier record.

## Smallest lawful remediation sequence

1. define separately versioned typed case, mutation, expected-result and
   reusable immutable fixture contracts with pure validation;
2. implement a deterministic pure case executor that derives findings and
   refuses missing, duplicate or unsupported cases without accepting booleans;
3. seal individual results, the ordered result-set digest and a read-only audit
   receipt; add a bounded value-aware secret detector and explicit detector
   scope;
4. recompute the complete canonical case matrix in the terminal audit rather
   than reading success prose; and
5. run the remediation adversarial audit, remove the qualification only if the
   recomputation passes, and close the campaign.
