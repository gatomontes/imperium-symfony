# Provider Binding Activation and Capability Custody campaign ready

## Completed foundation

Governed Tool and Provider Separation is terminal through Batch 9 under
`docs/handoffs/governed-tool-provider-separation-campaign-complete.md`. Its terminal audit proves the
provider-neutral inactive corridor, sterile second-adapter replaceability and removal of the
self-authorizing live command.

The audit also records the exact blockers: provider-binding activation authority is absent, and the
current environment-backed opaque capability cannot leave the issuing process without reissuance or
reconstruction.

is authorized.

Preparation must inventory and classify the exact activation authority, activation record or lease,
capability issuer, custody owner, cross-process delivery mechanism, pre-I/O atomic consumption,
crash checkpoints, replay and contention refusal, expiry, revocation, recovery, reconstruction and
secret-exclusion boundary. It must propose the smallest safe sequence and may conclude that the
environment-backed broker cannot support truthful cross-process custody.

## Preserved perimeter

Do not define runtime contracts, activate a binding, issue or resolve credentials, migrate the live
command, invoke a provider, perform external I/O, open Iron Gate or Lazaretto, or change inbound
webhook, sortie, credential-platform, revocation, propagation, telemetry, reassessment, containment
or incident behavior. Provider Execution Assurance remains paused.
