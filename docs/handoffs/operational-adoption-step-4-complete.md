# Handoff: operational-adoption Curial composition and assessment commissioning Step 4 complete

The presiding Seneschal resolves exactly one qualified active Curialis occupancy for each Step 3 jurisdiction: evidence sufficiency, mission and operational fit, and risk/authority/reversibility. Each target must be an explicitly classified `LEGATE` or `DELEGATE`, carry a sealed qualification reference, occupy a unique `curia.curialis.*` Seat, and be the sole active occupant of that Seat.

Curia issues three independently sealed commissions at `ISSUED_PENDING_CURIALIS_ACCEPTANCE`. The aggregate checkpoint is `LEGATE_RESULT_ADOPTION_CURIAL_COMPOSITION_RESOLVED_ASSESSMENT_COMMISSIONS_ISSUED_PENDING_ACCEPTANCE`.

Issuance is not recipient acceptance. Every commission remains non-exercisable and grants no assessment, cognition, provider, credential, tool, operational-use, adoption, planning-amendment, external-action, execution, or continuing-turn authority. The accepted result and closed source commission remain unchanged.

Step 5 may allow each exact targeted Curialis independently to accept or refuse only its own commission. No assessment authority may exist until the applicable recipient acceptance is sealed.
