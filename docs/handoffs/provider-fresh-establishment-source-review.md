## Explicit remaining proof boundary

The implementation and required full local gate are reported separately from complete R4 proof. Dedicated two-process Operator issuer/act/trust-expiry and founding-holder invalidation orderings, every alias/second-policy reset variant, and individual end-to-end invocation of all indirect consumers are not established by the selected test map. Serial issuer/policy revocation and authorization expiry, both Formation decision revocation/native enrollment orders, generic installer/seal orders, all eighty named publication crash boundaries, and exact pending recovery have concrete candidate cases. The final report records their actual outcomes and platform errors; neither this ledger nor a passing packet-integrity verifier upgrades missing proof to acceptance.

The native Operator envelope is separate from O2 admitted acts. Its nonce is checked against the existing revocation index, but this candidate supplies no separate admission owner for revoking that native envelope individually: AuthorityAdmission\Admission accepts an act target only when it occurs in the retained O2 acts map. Pending establishment can be stopped through the actual Formation decision revocation or Operator issuer revocation owners. Individual native Operator-act revocation is an implementation boundary, not merely a missing race test; the existing O2 allowlist and admission contract remain unchanged.

R3 substantive predicates, native assignment verification/application/replacement/use and full race/refusal proof remain open; R5 production ports remain missing and R6 commissioning/interview remains deferred.
