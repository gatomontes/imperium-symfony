## Governing taxonomy

- `Officer` is the umbrella.
- `LEGATE` identifies a permanent Office-bound Officer.
- `DELEGATE` identifies a temporary examination-, proceeding-, commission-, or mission-bound Officer.
- Classification grants no authority.
