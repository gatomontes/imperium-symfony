# Courtyard and Courtthane — owner-selected identity boundary

Status: selected for local implementation in this conversation. This amends the
reception/formation identity allocation in
[the earlier decisions](citadel-mission-formation-decisions.md); it does not
rewrite that record or assert that the runtime has already changed.

## Institutional meaning

| Name | Meaning and responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Citadel | Enclosing institutional jurisdiction; retain genuine Citadel identity and boundaries |
| Courtyard | Reception and mission-formation function within the Citadel |
| Courtthane | Permanent Office-bound Legate of the Courtyard; reception, relevance judgment, interview, understanding and separately authorized proposal work |
| Castellan | Reserved future Citadel oversight office; no oversight appointment, runtime service or powers are introduced here |
| Curia | Mission-specific body constituted through legitimate authority after exact proposal approval |
| Seneschal | Leads its Curia and assesses the incoming handoff; no automatic repetition of the initial interview |

Courtthane is the selected title; Yardreeve was considered and not selected.
The existing formation interview role moves from Castellan to Courtthane.
Seneschal and Isolde retain their Curia responsibilities. No new Secretariat,
provisional Curia, root principal, institution hierarchy or multi-Citadel feature
is created by this correction. Citadel is a jurisdiction, not itself an Officer.

## Preserved operating sequence

1. Courtyard receives and records the request without creating a Curia.
2. Exact existing-mission inquiries route mechanically. Courtthane discusses
   intent and semantic overlap with the Imperator within lawful visibility.
   Similarity alone does not veto independently authorized work.
3. Attributable understanding closes interview authority and preserves dissent.
4. Courtthane presents the exact bounded drafting request and asks:
   **“I understand. I am ready to draft a proposal. Do you approve?”**
5. Separate exact approval permits proposal drafting within disclosed scope,
   resource bounds and expiry. Readiness, silence and understanding do not.
6. Courtyard presents the numbered proposal. Material amendments obtain the
   applicable decision; unchanged authorized actions need no repeat approval.
7. Separate exact mission approval precedes admission, legitimate constitution
   and appointments. Proposal approval alone supplies no missing typed authority.
8. Courtyard hands off the approved dossier, original exchange, understanding,
   rationale, dissent, overlap and authority lineage to the mission Curia.
9. The receiving Seneschal accepts responsibility or identifies a concrete gap
   under its own authority. Handoff acceptance grants no execution authority.
10. Existing non-executing Delegate Step 1 validation and later authority gates
    remain intact. Execution requires its own valid prerequisites.

## Identity is not authority

Naming Courtthane does not appoint or qualify an occupant. Existing signed
`citadel.castellan` Profile targets, approvals and appointment effects cannot be
edited, resealed or interpreted as fresh Courtthane qualification. New Courtthane
work must have exact legitimate evidence for its selected Seat. Synthetic acts
are permitted only in disposable offline proofs.

Keep genuine Citadel IDs, registry ownership, parent/child lineage and institutional
authority distinct from Courtyard function and Courtthane Seat. Do not introduce
a second registry, journal, lock domain, budget or replay namespace through an
alias. Historical Castellan interview records confer no future oversight powers.

Historical reads, exact retained completion and recovery must remain attributable
to the original bytes and authority boundary. Compatibility may retain old wire
names or route CLI spellings to one implementation; it must never translate a
signed effect, promote an old appointment or renew a consumed/expired grant.
An old active workflow unsupported by the new identity must refuse fresh use with
a concrete reason while preserving evidence, reservations and recovery.
