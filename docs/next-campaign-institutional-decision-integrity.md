# Next campaign: Institutional Decision Integrity

## Purpose

Imperium must make consequential human authorization both informed and defensible. The decision-maker must receive the materially relevant options and consequences, while the runtime must preserve exactly what was decided, by whom, from which evidence, under which authority, and what remained unauthorized.

This campaign joins two existing proposals into one bounded program:

- institutional informed consent defines the decision surface presented to the Imperator; and
- the defensible decision record seals the resulting decision and its authority lineage.

Neither half is sufficient alone. A complete record of an impoverished choice is not informed consent. A well-explained choice without a canonical sealed decision record is not defensible governance.

- Credential-boundary remediation is terminal through Batch 17.

This campaign creates neither Delegate Mission Step 70, Runtime Integrity Hardening Step 36, nor Credential Boundary Batch 18.

## Constitutional boundary

The campaign may standardize how a decision surface and decision record are assembled, validated, sealed, persisted, replayed, superseded, and presented. It may not merge institutional actors or transfer their judgment.

- Curia discovers and explains materially relevant options; it does not decide for Imperator.
- Imperator selects, refuses, opposes, or requests modification; that decision is not inferred from silence.
- Machinery validates completeness, attribution, evidence, time, lineage, and authority effects; it does not invent rationale or choose an option.
- Existing Senate, Guildhall, Garrison, Conscription, Clavium, and La Cortine judgments remain independently owned.
- Historical sealed artifacts are never rewritten.
- Consent grants only the exact authority stated in the sealed decision record.
- Changed material facts invalidate reliance on prior consent and require re-presentation.

## Campaign sequence

### Batch 1 — canonical contracts

Define separately versioned contracts for:

1. an institutional decision surface; and
2. a defensible decision record.

Bind options presented, unavailable or prohibited options, material consequences, risks, reversibility, recommendation, evidence, decision owner, disposition, granted authority, denied authority, resulting state, rationale, time, limitations, expiry, and everything remaining unauthorized.

No existing lifecycle adopts the contracts in Batch 1.

### Batch 2 — mechanical validation and persistence

Implement canonical validators, digest sealing, immutable persistence, exact replay, conflicting-reuse refusal, and supersession lineage. Machinery must reject omission, ambiguity, stale evidence, incompetent residual-risk ownership, and context-free authorization prompts.

No cognitive component gains decision authority.

### Batch 3 — decision-surface assembly

Implement a mechanical assembler that constructs a candidate decision surface from exact sealed sources while preserving Curia's authored explanation and recommendation. Detect materially omitted options and distinguish unavailable, prohibited, rejected, and unexamined alternatives.

This batch must prove that silence, inactivity, familiarity, and prior consent are non-authorizing.

### Batch 4 — first bounded adoption

Adopt the pair at one existing Imperator authorization boundary selected after inspecting current contracts and tests. Prefer the personnel-use decision because its authority grant and downstream consumer are already explicit.

Preserve the existing decision-maker, dispositions, checkpoints, error vocabulary, and downstream authority. The new records accompany and bind the judgment; they do not replace or reinterpret it.

### Batch 5 — material-change and reauthorization

Define the exact fingerprint whose change makes consent stale. Prove that changed options, consequences, evidence, risk, scope, limitation, expiry, recipient, or requested authority cannot inherit a prior decision.

Return stale matters for a fresh decision surface without automatically granting continuation authority.

### Batch 6 — proof and campaign closeout

Prove:

- all required fields are mandatory;
- omitted material options fail stopped;
- ambiguous disclosure fails stopped;
- residual risk requires a competent named owner;
- identical replay returns the exact record;
- conflicting replay and changed material facts fail stopped;
- the underlying proceeding and evidence are mechanically reconstructable; and
- the adopted lifecycle remains behaviorally and constitutionally unchanged apart from the stronger decision evidence.

Update canonical documentation and prepare the next separate campaign only after these proofs pass.

## Explicit exclusions

This campaign does not implement:

- Iron Gate lease revalidation;
- sortie execution or sortie receipts;
- Lazaretto admission;
- revocation or kill-switch propagation;
- telemetry or anomaly detection;
- incident management;
- system-wide migration of every institutional decision;
- a new provider, model, credential source, or credential platform;
- new Senate findings, Guildhall suitability judgments, or Curia mission judgments.

Those belong to later continuous-governance or perimeter campaigns.

## Completion criterion

The campaign is complete when one consequential existing Imperator boundary cannot authorize from an incomplete, ambiguous, stale, unattributed, or evidentially unsupported decision surface, and the resulting sealed record proves the exact authority granted and denied without changing the competent actor or widening downstream authority.
