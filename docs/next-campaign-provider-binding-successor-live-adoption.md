# Next campaign: Provider Binding Successor Live Adoption

## Selection

Provider Binding Successor Live Adoption is separately selected after Provider
Binding Successor Production Realization closed at
`PROVIDER_BINDING_SUCCESSOR_PRODUCTION_REALIZATION_CAMPAIGN_COMPLETE_PRE_PROVIDER_EFFECT_ONLY`.

Only Provider Binding Successor Live Adoption Preparation Batch 0 is authorized.

The realized authority-empty contracts, validators and adversarial proof are
prerequisite evidence. They are not live-adoption authority. The provider
binding remains BOUND_INACTIVE and v3 execution admission remains
NOT_IMPLEMENTED.

## Preparation Batch 0 inventory

Preparation Batch 0 must inventory and classify:

- the exact competent live-adoption decision owner and executor principal;
- canonical decision issuance and immutable provenance;
- the single-use live-adoption authority issuer, custody and consumer;
- the exact completed successor, atomic-creation winner, adoption target and
  successor-to-v3 join required at entry;
- v3 execution-admission ownership, contract production and validation;
- whether v3 admission, authority consumption, successor adoption and binding
  lifecycle transition require one atomic same-root winner;
- the original BOUND_INACTIVE implementation binding and the exact successor
  binding state after adoption;
- crash cuts before and after the immutable winner;
- replay, changed evidence, same-root contention, expiry and revocation;
- read-only reconstruction and durable secret exclusion;
- process-local capability identity and credential non-authority;
- the final boundary before credential resolution, provider invocation,
  external I/O and provider effect start; and
- candidate boundary postures, refusals and deferred boundaries.

Every finding must be classified as `EXISTS_CANONICALLY`,
`EXISTS_FRAGMENTED`, `ABSENT` or `DEFERRED_BOUNDARY`.

## Batch estimate

The planning estimate is eight batches including Preparation Batch 0:

0. inventory and classification;
1. competent live-adoption decision and principal;
2. single-use live-adoption authority issuance and custody;
3. atomic v3 admission, authority consumption, adoption and binding transition;
4. interruption, replay, contention, expiry and revocation proof;
5. read-only aggregate reconstruction;
6. adversarial audit and secret-exclusion proof;
7. terminal audit and campaign closure.

This estimate grants no authority to skip a refusal or correction batch.
