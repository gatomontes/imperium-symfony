## Why this campaign exists

The post-terminal Blackquill review refuses canonical closure at
`NATIVE_INTEGRATION_TERMINAL_AUDIT_REFUSED_CANONICAL_CONSUMER_NOT_INTEGRATED`.

established downstream provider-execution/effect corridor. Eleven historical
descriptor readers retain separate meanings. A new route cannot prove canonical
integration merely by consuming itself.

The prior implementation remains the candidate substrate. This correction owns
only the missing downstream integration, bypass resistance and application-level
proof. It must not rebuild principal, successor, admission, journal or
reconstruction machinery unless Preparation Batch 0 proves a specific defect is
inseparable from the consumer join.

Only Preparation Batch 0 may next be considered.

## Success boundary

The campaign may close only if one identified established downstream entrypoint:

- obtains effective operation binding through a canonical interpretation
  boundary that consumes `NativeBindingReader` or a justified successor to it;
- cannot fall back to the raw `BOUND_INACTIVE` descriptor when native journal,
  transition, retirement or unknown state exists;
- distinguishes untouched inactive, committed-current, committed-not-current,
  incomplete, corrupt and unrelated-operation states;
- preserves the original descriptor and all effect prohibitions;
- is exercised through the Symfony container and real CLI/application wiring;
  and
- proves that every inventoried competing direct-descriptor route either uses
  the canonical interpretation or remains explicitly outside this operation.

A passing service-level test of the new island is insufficient.
