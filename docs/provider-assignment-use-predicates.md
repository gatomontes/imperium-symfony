## Identity, currentness and work bounds

Original references retain schema/identity/full digest, immutable Profile ID/version/content digest, exact evidence envelope digests, full approval bytes, native actor installation/binding/occupancy identity, designation-event generation, binding generation, target holder generation and application generation. They are distinct counters. A predecessor finding or approval is not a successor's finding or approval. New successor authorization/seal/Profile evidence is examined, approved and qualified independently in the real fixture before designation.

Owner validation precedes state access: fixed AuthorityStore object identity, live same-root FormationOwnerFrame, then parent/Formation identity. Detached calls, a second store instance and expired owner capabilities have direct reached refusals. Foreign journal/root and native currentness mutation permutations at assignment/use remain open.

Direct NativeAssignmentEvidence::verifyInOwner now opens the existing StrictJson::within scope before calling its private verifier. Application and settings already establish that scope and reuse it without nesting a cache lifetime or owner lock. Only exact pure parse/canonical results are reused, within the unchanged R2 limits (128 decoded entries / 2 MiB decoded budget); authority decisions, signatures and currentness are not cached. Cleanup remains in StrictJson's existing finally boundary. The compound direct-reader diagnostic exceeded its initial 90-second test cap both before and after the scope correction; both failures remain recorded. The final process test uses a 300-second cap for store reconstruction, native verification and two separate role reads. The scope correction establishes bounded parser lifetime, not a wall-clock performance guarantee. No runtime input/history/cache limit or hosted 30-minute limit changes. It does not define `profile.fits` or admit sources.

Existing source limits: StrictJson 1 MiB per input and depth 32; Admission at most 256 support records, 4 MiB bundle, depth 16 and 8,192 source visits, 4,096 admissions and 8,192 stored sources; AssignmentRule at most 256 pair sets / Profile evidence rows; ApplicationOwner at most 256 applications. Designation limits: 256 delegations/events/current entries, 16 MiB designation state, 256 KiB each personnel original, 1 MiB each native original and 32 MiB cumulative referenced history. These are source facts, not a PPC9 proof of every overflow/refusal branch. No pruning, parser-limit change, history reset or bound widening occurs. The requested complete native assignment/use bounds and corruption matrix remains open.
