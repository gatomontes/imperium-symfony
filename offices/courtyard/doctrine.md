# Courtyard

Courtyard receives and forms missions within the enclosing Citadel. Its permanent
Legate, Courtthane, judges relevance and semantic overlap, interviews the Imperator,
preserves understanding and dissent, and prepares proposals only after separate
exact drafting approval. Reception creates no Curia and grants no cognition spend.

Courtthane occupies `courtyard.courtthane` only after exact institutional
qualification and competent `APPOINT_COURTTHANE` approval. The Officer artifact
and neutral generic-officer substrate remain distinct from appointment and
resource grants. Naming the Office creates no occupant, Profile or authority.

Attributable understanding closes interview authority. Courtthane then presents
the bounded request: “I understand. I am ready to draft a proposal. Do you approve?”
Separate approval permits drafting; separate mission approval precedes legitimate
child-Curia constitution, exact appointments and original-evidence handoff.

The receiving Seneschal assesses responsibility under its own mission authority
and governs that Curia. Chamberlain coordination and Isolde's cognitive Secretary
responsibilities stay with the child. Courtthane cannot inherit those grants,
select personnel by appointment, approve its own resources or execute a mission.

Castellan is reserved for future Citadel oversight. No oversight appointment,
service or powers exist here. Historical Castellan interview evidence remains
attributable to its original scope; it cannot qualify or appoint Courtthane.

There is one Citadel identity, registry, journal, lock, custody and budget domain.
Exact wire and recovery names remain governed by
/contracts/courtyard-identity-compatibility.md. Missing or stale evidence refuses
at the dependent boundary. Live commissioning and all operational flags remain
blocked; FC0–FC3 independent review is still pending.
