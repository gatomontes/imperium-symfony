## Constitutional distinction

```text
Studium ≠ Chancellor ≠ Notary ≠ chamber ≠ doctrine artifact
```

Chancellor presides over Studium. Notaries perform separately attributable doctrinal work under specialization-bound Seats and Profiles. They are not a committee. Applicability, Boundary, Conduct, Evidence, Conflict, and Issuance are functional chambers, not presumed Officers.
