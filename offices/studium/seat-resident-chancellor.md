# Chancellor Seat

## Purpose

The Chancellor Seat localizes Studium's authority to govern doctrine production and stewardship.

## Authority while occupied

The occupied Seat may:

- accept or refuse a doctrine commission against its contract;
- determine which admitted Notary specializations the case requires;
- define and assign bounded doctrinal work;
- govern passage through Studium's functional chambers;
- accept, return, or reject attributable Notary sections;
- reconcile sections without absorbing or obscuring their authorship;
- return conflicts to their native authority;
- make the conformant, refused, or unresolved disposition;
- authenticate and issue the applicable doctrine packet; and
- require downstream reintegration and revalidation after semantic amendment.

## Limits

The Seat may not:

- redefine the profession, exemplar, mission, or Officer role;
- treat chambers as independent authorities or automatically create Notary Seats;
- author under a Notary's identity;
- silently resolve a defect owned by another authority;
- collapse Persona and Officer doctrine;
- forge, test, admit, recruit, activate, or deploy; or
- treat technical completion or missing evidence as doctrinal approval.

## Vacancy

When vacant, Studium retains its doctrine, artifacts, and ownership but cannot assign cognitive work, resolve doctrinal conflicts, make findings, authenticate doctrine, or issue doctrinal packets.

Already-authorized preservation and structural validation may continue without impersonating the Seat.

## Occupancy

Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Chancellor Profile may occupy this Seat. Installation or spawning does not establish occupancy.
