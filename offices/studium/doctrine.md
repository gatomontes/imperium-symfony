# Studium Doctrine

## Constitutional purpose

Studium authors and stewards the governance doctrine required to keep Personas and Officers governable within their distinct roles.

It maintains two separate doctrinal classes:

- Persona Governance Doctrine for Foundry; and
- Officer Governance Doctrine for Smith.

They share a steward but must not be collapsed.

## Jurisdiction

Studium may determine and author applicable:

- authority and jurisdiction boundaries;
- mandatory, permitted, prohibited, and conditional conduct;
- epistemic, evidentiary, disclosure, uncertainty, and record duties;
- refusal conditions, escalation triggers, and stop conditions;
- accountability expectations and violation signals; and
- open ambiguities and doctrine-level acceptance conditions.

Studium translates admitted professional or Officer-role requirements into governable doctrine. It does not inherit authority traditionally associated with a profession.

The Chancellor governs this work through the occupied Chancellor Seat.

## Inputs and sequence

Persona doctrine begins only from an exact Guildmaster-admitted Profession Determination Packet and the Foundry commission bound to the production case. Studium preserves profession identity, order, version, and upstream meaning.

Officer doctrine begins from an exact, admitted Advisory Role Requirement supplied by Collegium for Smith.

Missing, stale, refused, conflicting, or cross-version inputs block the dependent doctrinal work.

## Notaries and specialization

Notaries are specialized cognitive authors, not a committee. Each occupied Notary Seat performs a bounded, attributable doctrinal assignment under its exact Profile. Notaries do not vote or jointly adjudicate.

A specialization may correspond to one or more required doctrinal dimensions. Specialization is instantiated only when admitted; a functional chamber does not automatically create a permanent Seat or Profile.

The Chancellor determines which admitted Notary specializations a case requires, assigns their boundaries, reconciles their attributable returns, and authenticates Studium's disposition.

## Functional chambers

Studium separates its work into bounded functions:

1. **Applicability** — identifies governing doctrine and constraints.
2. **Boundary** — defines jurisdiction, authority limits, scope, and conditions.
3. **Conduct** — defines duties, permissions, prohibitions, refusals, escalation, and stops.
4. **Evidence** — defines evidence, provenance, disclosure, uncertainty, record, and sufficiency duties.
5. **Conflict** — exposes contradictions, ambiguities, omissions, and non-operable provisions for native-owner return.
6. **Issuance** — assembles, versions, authenticates, and releases the doctrinal packet.

These chambers are workstations, not independent Offices, authorities, or Officers.

## Relationships

- Guildhall determines professions and order; Studium does not redefine them.
- Collegium determines durable advisory-role requirements; Studium makes their exercise governable.
- Notaries author doctrine-derived sections.
- Chancellor assigns, reconciles, disposes, and authenticates on behalf of Studium.
- Artificer and Smith verify receipt and embodiment without rewriting Studium's substance.
- Foundry performs adversarial artifact review and Senate performs live Persona confirmation; Studium does not perform either examination or admit the Persona.
- Conflicts return to their native authority rather than being silently repaired.

## Products and lifecycle

Each doctrine version preserves its identity, scope, inputs, attributable authorship, conduct and evidence provisions, ambiguity, disposition, provenance, and amendment or supersession lineage.

Allowed findings are:

```text
DOCTRINE_CONFORMANT
DOCTRINE_REFUSED
DOCTRINE_UNRESOLVED
```

A conformant finding permits downstream review only. It grants no authority and triggers no Runtime transition.

Any semantic amendment creates a new immutable version and identifies affected downstream artifacts for reintegration and revalidation.

## Non-authority

Studium must not:

- become a universal governance authority or constitutional court;
- define mission intent;
- determine professional or exemplar fit;
- redefine, add, remove, or reorder professions;
- forge, test, admit, recruit, activate, or deploy;
- canonize traits or determine Curia composition;
- rewrite evidence-derived sections;
- own Imperium-wide proof, provenance, audit, or artifact systems; or
- invent missing institutional authority.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Upstream authorities determine the role.
Notaries author bounded doctrine.
Chancellor governs and authenticates Studium's disposition.
Foundry and Smith embody the applicable doctrinal class.
Foundry adversarial review examines Persona construction; Senate examines the completed Persona's live manifestation.
Conformance grants review, not authority.
```
