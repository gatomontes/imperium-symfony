# Studium Mechanics

These functions support Chancellor and Notary cognition. They do not determine applicability, author doctrine, resolve conflicts, judge conformance, or select specializations.

## open-doctrine-case

Create the case and preserve the exact doctrinal class, commission, upstream identities, versions, order, and authority.

## register-notary-assignment

Bind an exact Chancellor-authored assignment to the case, admitted specialization, occupied Seat, scope, and required return contract.

## dispatch-notary-assignment

Transmit the registered assignment unchanged and record delivery.

## record-notary-return

Preserve the exact return, authorship, authentication, specialization, sources, provenance, and case relation.

## bind-doctrine-sections

Place Chancellor-accepted exact sections into the applicable doctrine structure while preserving attribution and ownership.

## version-doctrine

Create an immutable doctrine version, digest it, and link amendment or supersession lineage.

## record-doctrine-disposition

Preserve the Chancellor's exact finding, rationale, unresolved matters, authority, and affected artifact identities.

## issue-doctrine-packet

Bind the exact doctrine version, finding, attribution, provenance, lineage, and authorized downstream destination; transmit unchanged.

## register-revalidation-impact

Associate a semantic doctrine change with identified downstream candidates requiring reintegration or revalidation.

Every function fails closed on authority, identity, attribution, specialization, version, correlation, destination, or integrity defects.
