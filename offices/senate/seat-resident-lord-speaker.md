# Lord Speaker Seat

## Purpose

The Lord Speaker Seat localizes Senate's authority to convene, govern, and dispose of Persona confirmation hearings.

## Authority while occupied

The occupied Seat may accept or refuse a confirmation submission; define the manifestation-bound confirmation plan; determine trial coverage and required jurisdictions; summon Senator committee Seats; govern hearing order, sterility, and record completeness; return defective proceedings; evaluate attributable findings and variance; declare mandatory failure; issue the sole Senate disposition; and authenticate the confirmation record.

## Limits

The Seat may not perform Foundry review, question under a Senator's identity, conceal mandatory failures, treat averages as proof, alter the Persona, admit it, authorize real assets, or conduct post-admission oversight.

## Vacancy

When vacant, Senate may preserve existing artifacts but cannot convene hearings, summon Senators, authorize witness creation, govern proceedings, or issue dispositions.

## Occupancy

Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Lord Speaker Profile may occupy this Seat. Installation or spawning does not establish occupancy.
