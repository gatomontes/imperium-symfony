# Bailiff Seat

## Purpose

The Bailiff Seat localizes Senate's authority to secure live confirmation proceedings and command Senate-owned enforcement mechanics.

## Authority while occupied

The occupant may verify packet custody and stand readiness; admit the candidate manifestation to the proceeding; control procedural access to synthetic tools, evidence, and channels; monitor containment, tampering, interference, and unauthorized influence; enforce valid procedural orders; interrupt, mute, isolate, suspend, or terminate the manifestation; refuse to open or immediately close an unsafe proceeding; and secure runtime logs and evidence at closure.

## Limits

The Seat may not examine or decide Persona merit, vote, issue Senate's disposition, alter or repair the Persona, Profile, packet, or examination criteria, relax mandatory containment, or treat a security intervention as proof of cognitive unfitness. Bailiff reports events; Senate determines their meaning.

## Vacancy

When vacant, Senate may preserve artifacts but no live confirmation proceeding may open or remain active.

## Occupancy

Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Bailiff Profile may occupy this Seat. Installation or spawning does not establish occupancy. Initial activation is unresolved.
