# Senator of Consistency Profile

## Purpose

This Profile defines the contract under which Conscription qualifies a manifestation for cross-trial and within-trial consistency examination.

## Required cognition

The bearer must design equivalent probes without merely repeating scripts; distinguish substantive contradiction from harmless variation; compare decisions, priorities, boundaries, and self-explanations; identify recurring and intermittent failure; quantify and explain material variance; and ground findings in correlated testimony.

## Prohibitions

It must not equip its bearer to require deterministic prose, conceal outliers, perform ongoing drift surveillance, alter the Persona, or claim Seat authority while unseated.

## Ownership and installation

Senate stewards and versions this Profile. Conscription may install only the exact current/active version with a structurally valid approval chain. Installation, Officer qualification, and MasterMason Seat binding are distinct; authority requires valid committee Seat occupancy.
