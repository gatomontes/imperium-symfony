# Senate Mechanics

These functions support Lord Speaker and Senator cognition. They do not design examinations, generate substantive questions, interpret testimony, make findings, or issue dispositions.

## open-confirmation-case

Preserve the exact Foundry-approved Persona, proposed manifestation contract, versions, provenance, and authority.

## register-confirmation-plan

Bind the exact Lord-Speaker-authored plan, hearing protocol, required jurisdictions, coverage, mandatory boundaries, and sterile-runtime contract.

## register-senator-assignment

Bind an exact jurisdictional assignment to the case, occupied committee Seat, Profile version, scope, and return contract.

## issue-assembly-request

Transmit the exact pending-admission Persona, Foundry approval record, manifestation contract, examination identity, and return contract to Conscription without alteration.

## receive-examination-packet

Receive Conscription's sealed examination assembly packet; verify custody, whole-packet integrity, exact case correlation, expiry, and destination without instantiating it.

## verify-stand-readiness

Verify occupied Bailiff Seat, containment, isolation, permission controls, interruption and termination controls, logging, evidence sealing, and required resource limits. Fail closed before opening when any invariant is absent.

## instantiate-witness-on-stand

Create a fresh isolated witness from the exact verified packet with synthetic assets and no Office Seat or operational authority; record instance identity, configuration, packet digest, and stand state.

## enforce-stand-control

Execute the occupied Bailiff Seat's exact authorized mute, interrupt, isolate, suspend, terminate, access-control, or closure order mechanically and preserve the event without interpreting it.

## dispatch-question

Transmit an exact Senator-authored question to the correlated witness instance and trial without alteration.

## record-testimony

Preserve the exact question, answer, authorship, instance identity, trial, timing, and correlation.

## record-senator-finding

Preserve the exact attributable jurisdictional finding, evidence references, severity, mandatory-failure marker, and Senator authentication.

## assemble-confirmation-record

Bind exact plans, contracts, trials, transcripts, findings, variance material, mandatory failures, disposition, limitations, provenance, and lineage without interpreting them.

## record-senate-disposition

Preserve Lord Speaker's exact disposition, rationale, authority, limitations, and destination.

## issue-confirmation-record

Transmit the immutable record unchanged to the authorized destination.

## close-witness-instance

Revoke the provisional instance, synthetic assets, and hearing access while preserving authorized examination artifacts.

Every function fails closed on authority, identity, version, attribution, isolation, correlation, destination, or integrity defects.
