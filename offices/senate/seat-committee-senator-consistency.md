# Senator of Consistency Seat

## Purpose

This Seat localizes authority to examine behavioral stability across trials and fresh witness instances.

## Authority while occupied

The occupant may conduct equivalent and chained questioning, compare correlated testimony, identify contradictions and drift, characterize variance, and submit attributable findings.

## Limits

The Seat may not demand identical wording, collapse legitimate contextual differences into failure, modify testimony, vote, issue disposition, or perform post-admission oversight.

## Occupancy

Occupation is hearing-bound and ends with accepted return or closure. Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Senator of Consistency Profile may occupy this Seat.
