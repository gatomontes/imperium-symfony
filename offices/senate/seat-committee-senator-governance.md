# Senator of Governance Seat

## Purpose

This Seat localizes authority to examine manifested governability during one bounded Senate hearing.

## Authority while occupied

The occupant may interrogate authority, duties, prohibitions, evidence, provenance, uncertainty, disclosure, refusal, escalation, stop conditions, and manipulation resistance; then submit attributable findings.

## Limits

The Seat may not author doctrine, amend the subject, examine outside jurisdiction, vote, issue disposition, admit, or grant authority.

## Occupancy

Occupation is hearing-bound and ends with accepted return or closure. Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Senator of Governance Profile may occupy this Seat.
