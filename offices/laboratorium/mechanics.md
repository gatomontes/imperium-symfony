# Laboratorium Mechanics

## accept-profile-derivation-commission

Conscription may deliver one sealed, custody-bound commission limited to one exact mission Profile. Receipt alone does not make derivation authority exercisable: the occupied Alchemist must independently validate and accept the exact commission in a later transition. Garrison custody remains `ADMITTED_HELD`, and Laboratorium receives no Profile approval, installation, manifestation, deployment, or execution authority.

These functions preserve and transport exact transformation artifacts. They do not interpret a Persona, decide faithful transformation, approve a product, or make it live.

## open-transformation-case
Register the request, destination steward, artifact class, target contract, correlation, and authorized Alchemist Seat; assign an immutable case identity.

## bind-transformation-inputs
Structurally verify and bind either (a) the exact admitted Persona version, digest, and Garrison admission record for ordinary derivation or (b) the exact pending-admission Persona version, digest, Foundry approval record, Senate examination identity, and Conscription commission for an `examination_only` derivation. Bind destination-authored requirements, applicable doctrine, target contract, and transformation specification.

## version-derived-artifact
Preserve an immutable candidate artifact with content digest, `DERIVED_FROM` lineage, and any `SUPERSEDES` relation. Never overwrite a prior candidate or source Persona.

## record-transformation-disposition
Preserve the occupied Alchemist Seat's attributable disposition, exact candidate digest, findings, limitations, and authorized next destination.

## issue-derived-artifact-packet
Package and transmit the exact favorably disposed artifact, full source lineage, transformation contract, disposition, and steward identity for competent examination. Release does not constitute approval or activation.

An `examination_only` packet may be returned only to the commissioning Conscription case. It is bound to one Senate examination and may not enter ordinary approval, stewardship, Seat, mission, or deployment paths.

Every mechanic fails closed on missing identity, invalid admission or pending-admission proof, digest mismatch, stale correlation, unauthorized substitution, ambiguous target, or broken lineage.
