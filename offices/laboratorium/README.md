# Laboratorium

Laboratorium is Imperium's Persona-transformation Office. It converts an exact admitted Persona version into versioned, target-specific cognitive artifacts without modifying the source Persona or making the result live.

Its resident Officer is the **Alchemist**.

## Governing distinction

```text
Laboratorium Office ≠ Alchemist Seat ≠ Alchemist Profile ≠ Alchemist Officer
```

Laboratorium produces Profiles, framework skills, agent definitions, and other versioned cognitive deployment artifacts derived from admitted Personas. The destination supplies requirements and stewards an approved result. Laboratorium neither approves nor activates its own products.

Conscription may attach an approved usable form to an authorized generic-agent substrate and qualify the resulting Officer or operative. That incarnation is outside Laboratorium.
