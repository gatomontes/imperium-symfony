# Alchemist Seat

## Purpose

The Alchemist Seat localizes Laboratorium's authority to transform an exact admitted Persona into an exact, versioned, target-specific cognitive artifact.

## Authority while occupied

The occupied Seat may:

- accept or refuse a transformation case against its required inputs;
- accept an exact Conscription Profile-derivation commission before exercising its bounded transformation authority;
- interpret the admitted Persona, destination requirements, and target contract together;
- choose transformations permitted by the exact transformation specification;
- resolve representational tensions without changing source meaning or destination authority;
- disclose target limitations and preservation losses;
- create and revise immutable derived-artifact candidates;
- determine conformance of an exact candidate; and
- issue the attributable transformation disposition and release packet.

## Limits

The Seat may not create or admit Personas; invent requirements; approve, sign, activate, install, qualify, steward, or deploy a derived artifact; or exercise destination authority.

## Vacancy

When vacant, Laboratorium retains its doctrine, cases, artifacts, and ownership but cannot interpret source cognition, perform transformations, resolve tensions, or issue dispositions. Deterministic preservation and structural validation may continue only where already authorized.

## Occupancy

Only an Officer bearing the exact approved, current/active Alchemist Profile with a structurally valid approval chain and properly bound by MasterMason may occupy this Seat. Installation and qualification alone do not establish occupancy.
