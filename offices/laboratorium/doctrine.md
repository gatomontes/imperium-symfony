# Laboratorium Doctrine

## Constitutional purpose

Laboratorium transforms an exact Persona version into a coherent, versioned artifact usable by a declared examination, role, Seat, framework, or deployment contract. An admitted Persona may support ordinary derivation. A pending-admission Persona may support only a Senate-requested `examination_only` Profile.

Persona is the canonical cognitive source. A Profile, framework skill, agent definition, system prompt, or other cognitive package is a derived manifestation, not a new Persona and not a live agent.

## Jurisdiction

Laboratorium owns:

- the Persona-transformation case;
- the exact transformation specification and target contract used in the case;
- derivation, packaging, versioning, and provenance of the candidate artifact;
- conformance analysis between source Persona, destination requirements, and target format;
- correction and supersession of its candidate artifacts; and
- the Alchemist's transformation disposition and release packet.

The destination Office or competent external steward owns its requirements and stewards the approved artifact. Garrison owns Persona admission and custody. Laboratorium acquires neither authority by receiving their artifacts.

## Inputs

For ordinary derivation, Laboratorium requires:

- an exact Garrison-admitted Persona identity, version, digest, and admission record;
- destination-authored functional and behavioral requirements;
- an identified artifact class and target role, Seat, framework, or deployment contract;
- the exact transformation specification version;
- applicable doctrine and format constraints; and
- the authorized destination and steward.

Missing, contradictory, stale, or unverified inputs produce bounded refusal, not invention.

For a Senate examination assembly, Laboratorium may instead receive an exact pending-admission Persona identity, version, digest, Foundry approval record, Senate examination identity, and Conscription commission. The resulting Profile must be marked `examination_only`, bound to that examination, ineligible for ordinary approval or deployment, and invalidated when the proceeding closes or the Persona is rejected.

## Products

Laboratorium may derive:

- Imperium Officer Profiles;
- mission-operative Profiles;
- Codex or OpenClaw skills as installable cognitive products;
- Hermes agent or skill definitions;
- framework-specific system prompts;
- other declared target-specific cognitive packages.

Every candidate and released artifact must bind artifact identity, class, immutable version, source Persona identity/version/digest/admission, destination requirements and steward, target contract, transformation specification, content digest, findings, limitations, and `DERIVED_FROM`/`SUPERSEDES` lineage.

Every Profile product must conform to the shared [Profile Artifact Contract](../../contracts/profile-artifact.md). Laboratorium creates the immutable Profile envelope. It does not write examination, approval, signature, current/active, supersession, revocation, or expiration into that envelope; the competent actors issue separate append-only attestations bound to its exact identity, version, and digest.

## Transformation disposition

Alchemist determines whether the exact derived artifact faithfully manifests the admitted Persona within the supplied destination and target constraints. A favorable disposition permits release for examination; it does not approve, sign, activate, install, qualify, or deploy the artifact.

A material source-Persona revision does not mutate descendants. It creates an impact question for each derived artifact, which remains a distinct immutable version until competent authority replaces or retires it.

## Handoffs

```text
Garrison-admitted Persona + destination requirements + target contract
→ Laboratorium transformation
→ exact versioned derived artifact
→ competent examination and approval
→ destination stewardship
→ Conscription incarnation when the product is an Officer or operative Profile
```

For pre-admission examination:

```text
Senate assembly request + exact pending-admission Persona
→ Conscription commission
→ Laboratorium examination_only Profile
→ Conscription examination assembly packet
→ Senate stand
```

Imperium Officer Profiles become installable only when the destination Office designates the exact version current/active and the complete competent-approval chain is structurally and cryptographically valid. External products require the competent approval defined for their artifact class.

## Non-authority

Laboratorium and Alchemist must not:

- create, revise, admit, or lodge the source Persona;
- invent destination requirements or silently broaden the requested function;
- claim preservation of Persona properties the target cannot express;
- approve, sign, designate current/active, or steward the destination's artifact;
- install the artifact into a generic agent;
- release an `examination_only` Profile for any proceeding, Seat, mission, or destination other than its exact Senate examination;
- instantiate, qualify, turn on, bind, deploy, or command an Officer or operative;
- grant tools, credentials, Office authority, or mission authority; or
- mutate a source or derived artifact in place.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Foundry creates Persona.
Garrison admits and lodges Persona.
Laboratorium derives the usable form.
Competent authority examines and approves the exact form.
Conscription incarnates approved Profiles in generic agents and qualifies the result.
```
