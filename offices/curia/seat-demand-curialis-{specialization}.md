# Curialis Seat Demand — `{specialization}`

## Mission need

Identify the exact mission question or decision requiring this Curialis and the evidence that general Curia composition is insufficient.

## Required judgment

Declare:

- the specialization and jurisdiction;
- required evidence literacy;
- required experience or demonstrated reasoning;
- expected Curial contribution;
- conflicts and independence requirements;
- information, tool, and credential boundaries;
- start, stop, expiry, and replacement conditions; and
- the conditions proving the Seat unnecessary.

## Authority boundary

The Curialis advises and deliberates within this demand. Occupancy creates no Seneschal authority, authorization power, Office jurisdiction, tool permission, or execution authority.
