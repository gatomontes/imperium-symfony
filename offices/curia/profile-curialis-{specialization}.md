# Curialis Profile — `{specialization}`

## Qualification purpose

Qualify a manifestation against one exact mission-specific Curialis Seat Demand.

## Required proof

The Profile must bind:

- the exact admitted Persona version;
- the exact Seat Demand version and digest;
- the mission instance and permitted deliberative scope;
- required knowledge and evidence-handling capabilities;
- independence and conflict constraints;
- prohibited jurisdictions and actions;
- information and capability boundaries; and
- expiry, revocation, replacement, and retirement conditions.

## Prohibitions

Qualification as a Curialis does not qualify the manifestation as Seneschal, Chamberlain, Secretary, executor, or holder of another Office's authority.
