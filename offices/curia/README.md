# Curia

Curia is the mission-specific governing Office of one Imperium instance. It owns the planning proceeding, mission dossier, executive deliberation, mission decisions, and mission orchestration within the exact authority granted to that instance.

## Migration status

These files define the active architecture pinned by the executable bootstrap manifest. Curia is the single governing runtime; its resident Seats are Seneschal, Chamberlain, and the optional Secretary.

## Constituents

- [`doctrine.md`](doctrine.md) — Curia's purpose, jurisdiction, authorities, and boundaries.
- [`mechanics.md`](mechanics.md) — non-cognitive operations supporting Curial proceedings and continuity.
- [`seat-imperator.md`](seat-imperator.md) — the exceptional human-held authority Seat.
- [`seat-resident-seneschal.md`](seat-resident-seneschal.md) — Curia's executive Seat.

## Governing allocation

> **Imperator authorizes. Seneschal decides. Chamberlain coordinates. Isolde records and communicates. Curiales deliberate.**

Curia ≠ Imperator Seat ≠ Seneschal Seat ≠ Chamberlain Seat ≠ Secretary Seat ≠ Curial Seat ≠ Persona ≠ Profile ≠ manifestation.
