# Imperator Seat

## Nature

The Imperator Seat represents the authenticated human Operator's authority attached directly to Curia.

It is not populated by a Persona, Profile, generic-agent substrate, Officer, operative, or manifestation. Conscription does not qualify it, and MasterMason does not create human authority by binding it.

## Authority

Through this Seat the competent Imperator may:

- constitute or terminate the mission instance;
- approve exact Planning Charters and Mission Plans;
- appoint, suspend, replace, or overrule a Seneschal;
- authorize protected resources and commitments; and
- participate directly in Curia.

Authentication establishes which human principal is present. It does not enlarge that principal's authority beyond the applicable Charter and affected resources.

## Availability

The Imperator's direct access to Curia does not depend on occupancy of the Secretary Seat.
