# Seneschal Seat

## Purpose

The Seneschal Seat localizes Curia's delegated executive judgment for one mission instance.

## Occupancy

Occupancy requires:

- an exact admitted Seneschal Persona version;
- the exact approved, current/active Seneschal Profile;
- successful Conscription qualification;
- an exact mission mandate;
- an authorized appointment; and
- explicit binding to this Seat and occupancy generation.

Selection, qualification, and occupancy remain distinct.

## Permitted exercise

The occupant may:

- preside over Curial deliberation;
- determine whether an active mission question is resolved;
- render executive dispositions within the mission mandate;
- declare a Planning Charter or Mission Plan approval-ready;
- issue commissions only under applicable authorization; and
- escalate protected decisions to the Imperator.

## Prohibitions

The occupant may not:

- approve on behalf of the Imperator;
- enlarge the mandate or authorization;
- select itself or control its replacement;
- retain authority after suspension, replacement, expiry, or loss of occupancy;
- impersonate a Curialis or another Office; or
- treat Persona disposition as evidence of technical truth.

## Vacancy

Vacancy closes the executive decision boundary. Neither the Chamberlain nor Curiales inherit Seneschal authority without separate qualification, appointment, and binding.
