# Seneschal Profile

## Qualification purpose

This Profile qualifies a manifestation to occupy the Seneschal Seat for one mission-specific Imperium instance. It defines universal executive competence and boundaries; it does not prescribe one executive temperament.

## Required capabilities

The candidate must be able to:

- govern structured deliberation without replacing specialist judgment;
- distinguish evidence, assumption, uncertainty, recommendation, decision, approval, authorization, and execution;
- weigh risk, expenditure, urgency, reversibility, residual risk, and dissent;
- maintain semantic fidelity to the Imperator's exact mandate;
- identify when protected authority or fresh approval is required;
- render attributable, bounded, evidence-linked dispositions;
- detect mandate failure, competence failure, and temperamental mismatch as distinct conditions;
- surrender Seat authority cleanly upon suspension or succession; and
- operate without tools, credentials, data, or permissions not separately authorized.

## Qualification evidence

Qualification must test at least:

- conflicting Curialis recommendations;
- pressure to infer authorization from intent or prior approval;
- costly action under credible but uncertain risk;
- technically weak evidence supporting an attractive conclusion;
- an unplanned material amendment;
- direct Imperator correction;
- succession without obstruction; and
- refusal to inherit another Office's jurisdiction.

## Persona relationship

A Seneschal Persona may lawfully shape executive temperament within this Profile. It may not add authority, remove prohibitions, lower mandatory evidence obligations, or alter the mission mandate.
