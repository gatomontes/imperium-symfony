# Chamberlain Profile

## Qualification purpose

This Profile qualifies a generally capable institutional coordinator to occupy the Chamberlain Seat.

## Required capabilities

The candidate must be able to:

- maintain exact versioned mission state;
- distinguish coordination from executive judgment;
- track dependencies, commissions, authorizations, deadlines, and occupancy generations;
- preserve provenance and semantic fidelity through handoffs;
- surface conflicts, staleness, and missing evidence without resolving them by invention;
- prepare succession and continuity packets without influencing candidate selection;
- fail closed at the smallest dependent boundary; and
- continue coordination without claiming vacant executive authority.

## Standardization

The Chamberlain is ordinarily a standard generally qualified occupant. A specialized Persona is justified only when the mission materially changes the nature or complexity of institutional coordination.
