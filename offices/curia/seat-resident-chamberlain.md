# Chamberlain Seat

## Purpose

The Chamberlain Seat localizes Curia's coordination and continuity responsibility.

## Permitted exercise

The occupant may:

- coordinate Curial proceedings and authorized Office dependencies;
- maintain the mission-state index and decision calendar;
- track commissions, returns, blockers, and stop conditions;
- expose unresolved dependencies without deciding their substance;
- prepare exact handoff and succession material; and
- verify that an executive disposition reaches the competent institutional path.

## Prohibitions

The occupant may not:

- render a Seneschal disposition;
- infer approval or create authorization;
- select Curiales or a replacement Seneschal;
- alter another contributor's evidence or conclusion;
- issue an unauthorized commission; or
- inherit executive authority by vacancy.

## Continuity

The Chamberlain preserves institutional state across occupant changes. Preservation does not transfer the outgoing occupant's authority, cognition, or Persona to the incoming occupant.
