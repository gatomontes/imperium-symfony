# Foundry Mechanics

These functions support Artificer cognition and authority. They do not reconcile substance, judge coherence, conduct adversarial analysis, approve production, or decide repair ownership.

## demand-activation
When an exact construction authorization reaches a vacant Foundry, validate and preserve the delivery, then demand the canonical resident Artificer from MasterMason. Construction authority remains bound to the exact demands but cannot be exercised while the Artificer Seat is vacant. Activation does not require a mission-selected Persona and grants no spawning, Seat-binding, acceptance, or execution authority.

## open-production-case
Create the case, preserve exact inputs, and instantiate the admitted profession order.

## dispatch-commission
Bind an exact Artificer-authored commission to case, queue position, recipient, and authority; transmit unchanged.

## record-specialized-return
Preserve the exact return, authorship, authentication, sources, and case relation.

## bind-candidate-sections
Place Artificer-accepted exact sections into the tagged structure while preserving ownership and provenance.

## version-candidate
Create an immutable candidate version, digest it, and link any predecessor by `SUPERSEDES`.

## dispatch-for-adversarial-review
Bind an Artificer-authorized immutable candidate to identity, digest, template, provenance, review scope, and the occupied Adversarial Reviewer Seat; transmit unchanged.

## record-adversarial-return
Preserve the exact attributed review record and correlate it to the exact candidate version and digest.

## route-adversarial-return
Route the authenticated review return to Artificer without interpreting, weakening, or resolving its findings.

## record-production-disposition
Preserve the Artificer's exact approval or refusal, adversarial-review record, candidate identity, rationale, and authority.

## issue-release-packet
Bind the exact approved Persona, adversarial-review record, fingerprints, lineage, approval, and destination.

Every function fails closed on authority, identity, attribution, version, correlation, or integrity defects.
