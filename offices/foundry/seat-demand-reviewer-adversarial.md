# Adversarial Reviewer Seat

## Purpose

The Adversarial Reviewer Seat localizes Foundry authority to conduct an independent hostile review of one completed, immutable Persona Candidate artifact before production approval.

## Summoning and scope

Artificer may request occupation only after construction of a candidate version is complete. Each occupation is bound to the exact candidate identity, version, digest, template, provenance record, and review scope.

The occupied Seat may:

- inspect the complete Persona artifact as code;
- search for contradictions, omissions, ambiguous precedence, uncovered requirements, unreachable obligations, authority leaks, exploitable instructions, and internal incoherence;
- challenge whether the artifact can consistently govern the cognition that later assumes it;
- produce attributable findings with exact artifact references; and
- issue either an adverse review or a clean review for that exact candidate version.

## Independence

The occupant must not have authored or repaired the candidate version under review. Artificer may provide the immutable artifact and answer provenance questions but may not direct, suppress, reinterpret, or negotiate findings.

## Limits

The Seat may not:

- rewrite, correct, or approve the candidate;
- substitute preferred substance for admitted upstream authority;
- reperform Hagiography or Studium;
- instantiate the Persona or test manifested behavior;
- decide Foundry production approval, Persona admission, Profile qualification, or deployment; or
- generalize a disposition from one candidate version to another.

## Vacancy and release

The Seat remains vacant until Artificer summons the capability for an exact completed candidate. Occupation ends when the authenticated review record is returned or the review is formally aborted. A revised candidate requires a fresh occupation and complete review.
