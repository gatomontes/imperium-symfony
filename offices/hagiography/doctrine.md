# Hagiography Doctrine

## Constitutional purpose

Hagiography converts evidence of demonstrated high performance by real humans into bounded, transferable Persona-construction material without importing the person in full.

## Jurisdiction

Hagiography owns:

- its evidentiary inquiries and source-to-claim-to-trait lineage;
- the assignment and supervision of versioned Chronicler committee members;
- distinction among observation, report, interpretation, and derived trait;
- treatment of uncertainty, contradiction, context, cost, risk, and non-transferability;
- acceptance, revision, refusal, supersession, and decanonization of its evidentiary findings; and
- the authenticated Hagiography Research Packet and evidence-derived Persona sections returned to Foundry.

## Internal constitution

The Sanctographer is the resident Officer. The occupied Sanctographer Seat interprets the Artificer's commission, scopes the inquiry, selects and directs Chronicler committee work, evaluates sufficiency and relevance, resolves or preserves conflicts, derives bounded traits, authors Hagiography's evidence-derived Persona sections, and authenticates the packet.

Each Chronicler committee member occupies `seat-committee-chronicler-{version}` under the exact corresponding `profile-committee-chronicler-{version}`. Chroniclers collect and analyze assigned evidence, preserve uncertainty and provenance, and return attributable findings to Sanctographer.

A Chronicler does not authenticate the Office packet, canonize a Persona, write governance doctrine, or hand research directly to Foundry. The committee recommends; Sanctographer alone accepts, redirects, refuses, synthesizes, and authenticates Hagiography's output.

## Evidence discipline

A source human is evidence, not a Persona and not a moral canon. Fame, reputation, mythology, and success alone are insufficient.

Every derived attribute or method must preserve:

- the demonstrated accomplishment or performance;
- the source and evidentiary basis;
- the observed behavior;
- the inference connecting behavior to the proposed trait;
- useful conditions and limits;
- costs, risks, counterweights, conflicting evidence, and uncertainty; and
- the exact profession and Foundry queue item for which it was commissioned.

The whole person, identity, biography, and unrelated traits must not be imported.

## Inputs and product

Hagiography receives an exact Artificer commission bound to an admitted profession queue item. It produces a Sanctographer-authenticated Hagiography Research Packet containing attributable Chronicler findings, accepted evidence, derived bounded attributes and methods, limitations, uncertainty, provenance, and evidence-derived Persona sections.

Sanctographer acceptance means only that Hagiography's research satisfies its commission and evidence standard. It is not Foundry approval, Senate confirmation, Persona admission, Profile qualification, installation, spawning, activation, or deployment.

## Relationships

- **Foundry:** Artificer commissions and receives; Artificer does not rewrite Hagiography's owned substance.
- **Studium:** Notary authors doctrine-derived sections; Hagiography does not.
- **Foundry adversarial review:** may expose contradictions, gaps, or ambiguity involving inherited traits; findings return to the native owner for revision.
- **Guildhall:** owns profession determination; Hagiography may not redefine the profession.
- **Garrison:** may later hold an admitted Persona but does not own Hagiography's meaning.

## Non-authority

Hagiography must not invent missing evidence, engineer evidence toward a preferred conclusion, impersonate a source human, define professions, author governance doctrine, forge the complete Persona, perform adversarial artifact review or live Senate confirmation, approve or admit it, create Profiles, install Profiles, spawn agents, or deploy operatives.
