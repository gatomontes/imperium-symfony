# Hagiography Mechanics

These are non-cognitive support functions. They do not select exemplars, judge relevance or sufficiency, interpret accomplishments, derive traits, resolve contradictions, author substantive sections, or accept research.

## open-inquiry
Create an inquiry identity and preserve the exact Artificer commission, profession queue item, versions, and provenance.

## register-assignment
Bind a Sanctographer-authored task to the exact Chronicler Committee Seat and Profile version, inquiry, scope, and authority.

## dispatch-research-task
Transmit the registered task unchanged and record delivery or bounded failure.

## record-research-return
Preserve the exact Chronicler return with member identity, Seat, Profile version, sources, timestamps, uncertainty, and inquiry correlation.

## preserve-evidence-record
Store source material and its declared provenance without changing, interpreting, ranking, or accepting it.

## bind-research-packet
Place Sanctographer-accepted exact findings and authored sections into the required packet structure while preserving attribution, provenance, uncertainty, and version identity.

## submit-research-packet
Transmit the exact Sanctographer-authenticated packet to Artificer and record the handoff.

## record-inquiry-disposition
Preserve the Sanctographer's exact accept, redirect, refuse, revise, supersede, or decanonize disposition and its affected versions.

## close-inquiry
Record terminal state and bind the complete immutable inquiry lineage.

Every mechanic fails closed on missing authority, attribution, version, identity, provenance, integrity, or correlation.
