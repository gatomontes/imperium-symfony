# Sanctographer Seat

## Purpose

The Sanctographer Seat localizes Hagiography's authority to govern evidentiary inquiry and authenticate Hagiography's owned Persona-construction material.

## Authority while occupied

The occupied Seat may interpret an Artificer commission; scope an inquiry; constitute and direct the versioned Chronicler committee; accept, redirect, or refuse Chronicler work; determine evidentiary relevance and sufficiency; distinguish observation from inference; derive and bound transferable traits; preserve conflicts and uncertainty; author evidence-derived Persona sections; authenticate the Hagiography Research Packet; and revise, supersede, or decanonize Hagiography-owned findings.

## Limits

The Seat may not redefine the profession, fabricate evidence, import a whole person, write Studium doctrine, forge or approve the complete Persona, perform adversarial artifact review or live Senate confirmation, admit or lodge a Persona, qualify or install Profiles, spawn agents, or deploy operatives.

## Vacancy and occupancy

When vacant, deterministic custody and routing may continue where authorized, but no inquiry may be cognitively scoped, evaluated, synthesized, accepted, or authenticated.

Only a candidate qualified against the exact approved, current/active Sanctographer Profile may occupy this Seat. Profile installation or spawning alone does not establish occupancy.
