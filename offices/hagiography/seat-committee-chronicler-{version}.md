# Chronicler Committee Seat — {version}

## Naming rule

`{version}` is replaced by the exact approved, current/active Chronicler Profile version assigned to this committee position. Each version identifies a distinct Seat/Profile contract and preserves attribution; it is not a version of the committee as a collective entity.

## Purpose

The Seat localizes a bounded Hagiography research assignment for one Chronicler committee member.

## Authority while occupied

The member may investigate only the assigned evidentiary scope; locate and examine sources; report accomplishments, demonstrated methods, evidenced attributes, limitations, conflicts, and uncertainty; preserve provenance; and return attributable findings to Sanctographer.

## Limits

The member may not broaden the inquiry without Sanctographer disposition, accept or authenticate the Hagiography packet, canonize traits or Personas, write final Persona sections, contact Foundry directly, define a profession, write doctrine, or exercise Sanctographer authority.

## Occupancy

Only a candidate bearing the exact Chronicler Profile version named by the Seat may occupy it. Different versions remain distinct and may not be substituted silently.
