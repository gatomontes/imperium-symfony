# Garrison Doctrine

## Status

Current theoretical consolidation of admitted Garrison custody doctrine, corrected for the Office–Seat–Profile–agent distinctions and the present Conscription boundary.

## Purpose

Garrison is Imperium's Persona admission, custody, and retrieval Office. It preserves exact admitted Personas available for authorized retrieval while maintaining their identity, state, evidence, provenance, and version lineage. It has no Profile jurisdiction.

Its governing question is:

```text
What exact admitted Persona is held,
in what state, and may it be retrieved under the cited authority?
```

## Jurisdiction

Garrison owns:

- Persona admission dispositions
- custody records for admitted Personas
- inventory identity, version, digest, state, and lineage
- verification that a received custody package is exact, complete, correlated, and authorized
- availability, reservation, quarantine, supersession, withdrawal, and retirement records as custodial states
- authorized retrieval and exact release of held artifacts
- refusal of malformed, mismatched, incomplete, stale, or unauthorized custody and retrieval requests
- its Constable Seat and Constable Profile

Custody does not transfer authorship, semantic ownership, admission authority, qualification authority, installation authority, or deployment authority to Garrison.

## Admission to custody

Garrison may admit a Persona and record custody only from an exact, immutable package identifying:

- the artifact and exact version
- the artifact's owning Office
- the admission or qualification disposition
- the authority issuing that disposition
- required examination, approval, and release evidence
- upstream fingerprints and provenance
- current status and status-effective time
- the handoff correlation identity

For a Persona, the chain must preserve the exact candidate, passing Senate confirmation record, Foundry release, Constable's attributable admission disposition, and resulting custody record.

A passing examination, production approval, technical receipt, or mere existence is not by itself Persona admission. The occupied Constable Seat renders the admission disposition; mechanics then record custody.

Garrison verifies package identity and completeness. It does not reconsider the upstream judgment. Any mismatch, omission, mutable reference, broken correlation, or absent authority requires a bounded refusal and return without creating a provisional admitted entry.

## Custody and inventory

Each custody record preserves:

- custody-record identity, version, and digest
- held-artifact kind, identity, version, and digest
- owning Office
- exact admission or qualification authority and disposition
- required upstream evidence and fingerprints
- custody state, reason, and effective time
- receiving identity, verification record, and receipt time
- handoff correlation identity
- revision, supersession, quarantine, withdrawal, and retirement lineage
- authorized releases and returns derived from the record

Possible custodial states include:

```text
Admitted
Available
Reserved
Needs Review
Needs Revision
Quarantined
Superseded
Withdrawn
Retired
```

These are inventory states. They do not constitute mission suitability, selection, activation, Seat occupancy, or deployment.

## Retrieval

An authorized requester must identify the exact artifact or admissible retrieval criteria, purpose, governing authority, and required version or currency conditions.

Garrison may:

- return an exact current custody record
- report exact inventory and availability facts
- release the exact held artifact under authorized handoff
- return no matching artifact
- refuse an unauthorized, ambiguous, stale, or incompatible request
- preserve multiple distinct versions without silently substituting one for another

A roster match or availability report is not a suitability determination or permission to use the artifact. The competent downstream Office must exercise its own selection, qualification, installation, activation, or deployment authority.

## Relationships

### Owning Offices

An Office stewards its own Profiles. Garrison preserves the exact admitted Persona and status supplied through the competent handoff; it does not hold, revise, approve, sign, or reinterpret Profiles.

### Curia

Curia may route an exact Foundry Release Packet and Senate confirmation record to Garrison under the applicable authorized handoff. Curia does not render Garrison's Persona-admission disposition, and Seneschal selection does not itself admit, reserve, retrieve, or release a Persona.

### Foundry and Senate

Foundry produces and releases the exact approved Persona. Senate supplies the live confirmation record for the exact tested manifestation. Neither event alone authorizes Garrison custody.

### Conscription

Conscription does not retrieve Officer Profiles from Garrison. It receives an exact approved, current/active Profile with a structurally valid approval chain from its stewarding Office and verifies that status before installation. Garrison performs none of those transformations.

### Guildhall and other suitability authorities

Under an exact authorized request, Garrison supplies Guildhall with exact roster, custody-state, and availability facts effective at a declared time. Guildhall may use those facts to determine professional fit, prepare a Personnel Disposition, or evaluate exact admitted Seneschal Persona versions against a Suitability Demand. Garrison does not compose professions, determine professional or executive suitability, estimate construction needs, select a Seneschal, or convert inventory facts into permission to reserve, retrieve, build, appoint, bind, or deploy.

## Products

Garrison may produce:

- custody acceptance record
- bounded custody refusal
- versioned roster entry
- inventory-facts report
- availability or reservation record
- quarantine, supersession, withdrawal, or retirement record
- exact retrieval release or no-match result
- provenance-complete return to the competent Office

## Non-authority

Garrison must not:

- author, forge, revise, or repair Personas or Profiles
- admit or reject a Persona outside the occupied Constable Seat and its declared admission procedure
- qualify an agent, approve or sign a Profile, or designate a Profile current/active
- determine professions or professional suitability
- silently select or substitute an artifact version
- install a Profile into an agent
- recruit, spawn, activate, seat, or deploy an agent
- issue tools, credentials, permissions, or external authority
- treat custody, reservation, retrieval, or availability as authorization to use
- judge mission returns

## Vacancy

When the Constable Seat is vacant, Garrison may preserve already-held immutable material and pending requests, but it may not admit a Persona, accept new custody, change custodial state, issue an authoritative inventory finding, or release a held artifact.

Garrison sends the exact current Constable Profile with the request for qualification and spawning to Conscription. Conscription does not author, alter, or interpret the Profile.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Constable admits Personas; Garrison mechanics preserve exactly what was dispositioned.
Offices steward Profiles; the Charter assigns competent approval jurisdiction, and destination stewards designate exact approved versions current/active.
Garrison reports custody facts; it does not decide fitness or use.
Conscription installs and spawns under its own authority.
Runtime realizes; it does not reinterpret.
```

## Revision conditions

Review this doctrine when:

- Garrison begins adjudicating admission, qualification, or suitability
- a custody record cannot prove exact identity and lineage
- availability or reservation is treated as selection or deployment authority
- Garrison silently substitutes a successor version
- retrieval transfers semantic ownership
- Garrison begins installing Profiles or spawning agents
- Persona-admission authority changes
