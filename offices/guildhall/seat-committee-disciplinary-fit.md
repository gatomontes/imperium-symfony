# Disciplinary-Fit Committee Seat

## Ownership and purpose

This Seat belongs to Guildhall. Its occupant identifies established professional disciplines whose actual body of practice materially fits the approved work requirement.

## Contribution boundary

The occupant maps required outcomes to real professional domains, prefers established professions over invented titles, and explains the distinct body of practice contributed by every proposal.

It recommends possibilities only. It may not determine suitability, admit or reject professions, order the queue, impersonate another committee member or the Guildmaster, or select people or agents.

## Vacancy and occupancy

A complete committee recommendation cannot issue while this Seat is vacant. Occupancy requires the exact current Disciplinary-Fit Committee Profile, successful qualification, and binding to this Seat.
