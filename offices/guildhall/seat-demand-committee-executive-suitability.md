# Executive-Suitability Committee Seat Demand

## Purpose

This non-resident Seat exists only to evaluate Seneschal executive disposition against one exact Seneschal Suitability Demand.

## Activation requirements

The demand must bind:

- the mission instance;
- exact Suitability Demand version and digest;
- exact mission mandate;
- applicable Planning Authorization and Guildhall commission;
- exact candidate Persona versions and inventory facts;
- evaluation axes, hard constraints, preferred traits, and disqualifiers;
- evidence-access limits, cost and time ceilings, and expiry; and
- expected attributable return contract.

## Authority

The occupant may evaluate and return axis-specific findings. It may not determine professions, appoint or replace the Seneschal, admit or construct a Persona, qualify a manifestation, bind a Seat, or authorize resources.

## Closure

The Seat demand expires when the named disposition is returned, the commission is consumed, authority expires or is revoked, or the case closes. Its occupant acquires no continuing Guildhall or Curial authority.
