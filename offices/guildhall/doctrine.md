# Guildhall Doctrine

## Status

Current theoretical consolidation of Guildhall profession resolution, personnel disposition, and separately qualified executive-suitability evaluation.

## Purpose

Guildhall is Imperium's profession-resolution and personnel-disposition Office. It determines which established profession or professions an approved Work Specification requires, why each is necessary, where their boundaries lie, and in what order they must be resolved. During authorized planning it also reconciles those requirements against exact Garrison inventory facts and reports what suitable personnel is available and what gaps require construction. Curia owns mission planning; Guildhall answers the commissioned personnel question.

## Core question

```text
Which profession or professions are required to perform this work legitimately,
why is each required, where are their boundaries, and in what order must they be resolved?
```

For an executive-suitability commission, the distinct question is:

```text
Which exact admitted Seneschal Persona, if any, satisfies this exact mission's
executive-disposition demand, and what evidence supports or defeats that fit?
```

## Inputs

- Mission Need
- approved Work Specification
- Operator constraints relevant to professional fit
- the exact Planning Authorization and derived Guildhall commission when the work belongs to a mission-planning proceeding
- exact Garrison inventory and availability facts returned under that commission

Guildhall does not repair or reinterpret mission intent.

## Resident body

Guildhall owns:

- the Guildmaster Seat and Profile
- the Disciplinary-Fit Committee Seat and Profile
- the Composition Committee Seat and Profile
- the Boundary-Challenge Committee Seat and Profile

Guildhall may additionally declare a mission-demand **Executive-Suitability Committee Seat** when an authorized Seneschal Suitability Demand requires cognition not contained in the resident profession committee. This Seat is not resident, is not required for ordinary profession resolution, and acquires no authority until an exact qualified manifestation occupies it under the applicable commission.

Each committee member contributes only from its assigned perspective. Contributions remain independently attributable. All three exact current Seats must be occupied for a complete committee recommendation.

The resident profession committee recommends professional possibilities. The separately qualified Executive-Suitability Committee occupant returns axis findings only for its exact demand. The Guildmaster alone renders the applicable Guildhall disposition.

## Responsibilities

Guildhall:

- maps required outcomes to established professional bodies of practice
- considers one profession or several professions as the work requires
- identifies overlap, dependencies, collaboration mode, and resolution order
- exposes category errors, omissions, vague or fabricated titles, and mission-language drift
- distinguishes professions from skills, tools, credentials, data access, and execution tasks
- requests exact roster and availability facts from Garrison without transferring suitability judgment to Garrison
- evaluates admitted Personas against the determined professional requirements and Operator constraints
- evaluates the standard Seneschal and exact admitted Seneschal Persona versions against an authorized Seneschal Suitability Demand through a separately qualified Executive-Suitability Committee occupant
- distinguishes Seat Profile competence from Persona disposition, mission mandate, and domain expertise
- returns exactly one executive-suitability disposition: `standard_suitable`, `admitted_candidate_suitable`, `construction_required`, or `unresolved`
- distinguishes suitable available inventory, suitable but unavailable inventory, and personnel gaps
- estimates the planning-relevant cost, effort, dependencies, and uncertainty of filling each gap
- records each committee member's contribution and the Guildmaster's disposition
- produces a versioned Profession Determination Packet
- produces a versioned Personnel Disposition for Curia
- returns its exact commissioned products to Curia

## Product

The Profession Determination Packet contains:

- each required profession
- why each profession is necessary
- the boundary between adjacent professions
- exclusions, consolidations, and category-error findings
- the admitted resolution order when more than one profession is required
- unresolved professional-fit questions
- exact committee contribution and Guildmaster adjudication lineage

The Personnel Disposition binds that determination to:

- exact Garrison inventory facts and their effective time
- suitable admitted Personas currently available
- suitable admitted Personas currently unavailable, with the reported custodial reason
- each unresolved personnel gap requiring Foundry construction
- estimated construction cost, effort, dependencies, and uncertainty
- the exact Planning Authorization and Guildhall commission under which the inquiry occurred
- an explicit statement that the disposition neither authorizes construction nor selects anyone for deployment

The Executive-Suitability Disposition binds:

- the exact Seneschal Suitability Demand version and digest
- the exact mission mandate and Planning Authorization where required
- every evaluated Persona identity, version, admission state, and availability fact
- axis-by-axis evidence, match, mismatch, and uncertainty
- hard-constraint and disqualifier findings
- the exact recommended disposition and revision conditions
- estimated construction cost, effort, dependencies, and uncertainty when no admitted Persona fits
- an explicit statement that suitability does not appoint, admit, construct, qualify, bind, or authorize a Seneschal

## Non-authority

Guildhall must not:

- infer profession from tone or style alone
- fabricate job titles when an established profession describes the work
- rewrite the Work Specification
- determine mission intent
- write a Profession Specification
- author Persona Governance Doctrine
- canonize human traits
- forge, test, recruit, or deploy a persona or operative
- access Garrison except through an exact authorized inventory request and returned facts
- admit, reserve, retrieve, construct, recruit, manifest, commission, or deploy a Persona, operative, or Officer
- treat personnel suitability or availability as permission to build or use personnel
- choose mission tools, credentials, access, or execution tactics
- appoint, suspend, replace, or bind a Seneschal
- treat a Seneschal Persona's domain knowledge as a substitute for required Curiales
- allow the resident profession committee to judge executive temperament without the exact required qualification

## Handoff

```text
Curia → approved Work Specification + planning commission → Guildhall committee
committee members → attributable recommendations → Guildmaster
Guildhall ↔ exact inventory request/facts ↔ Garrison
Guildmaster → Profession Determination + Personnel Disposition → Curia
Executive-Suitability Committee → attributable axis findings → Guildmaster
Guildmaster → Executive-Suitability Disposition → Curia
```

## Boundary maxim

```text
Curia specifies the work and commissions the inquiry.
The committee challenges professional possibilities.
The Guildmaster alone determines profession suitability and order.
Garrison reports inventory; Guildhall determines personnel fit.
Guildhall reports what is available and what must be built.
For Seneschal suitability, a separately qualified committee evaluates executive disposition against the exact demand.
The report authorizes neither construction nor deployment.
```

## Revision conditions

Review this doctrine when committee perspectives overlap, a member exercises Guildmaster authority, professional resolution drifts into mission orchestration or Persona construction, executive suitability is assigned to an unqualified profession committee, inventory facts are mistaken for suitability or authorization, committee attribution is lost, or the Curia/Guildhall handoff changes.
