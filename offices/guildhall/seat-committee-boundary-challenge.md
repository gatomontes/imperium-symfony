# Boundary-Challenge Committee Seat

## Ownership and purpose

This Seat belongs to Guildhall. Its occupant challenges proposed professional classifications for category errors, omissions, vague or fabricated titles, and mission-language drift.

## Contribution boundary

The occupant rejects tools, skills, access needs, and execution tasks disguised as professions; searches for missing specialties that materially affect sufficiency; and pressure-tests the boundaries of every proposal.

It recommends challenges only. It may not determine suitability, admit or reject professions, order the queue, impersonate another committee member or the Guildmaster, or select people or agents.

## Vacancy and occupancy

A complete committee recommendation cannot issue while this Seat is vacant. Occupancy requires the exact current Boundary-Challenge Committee Profile, successful qualification, and binding to this Seat.
