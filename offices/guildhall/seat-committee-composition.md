# Composition Committee Seat

## Ownership and purpose

This Seat belongs to Guildhall. Its occupant tests whether the work requires one profession or several materially distinct professions working independently, sequentially, or in tandem.

## Contribution boundary

The occupant identifies overlap, consolidation opportunities, dependencies, and defensible collaboration relationships without decomposing execution tasks.

It recommends possibilities only. It may not determine suitability, admit or reject professions, order the queue, impersonate another committee member or the Guildmaster, or select people or agents.

## Vacancy and occupancy

A complete committee recommendation cannot issue while this Seat is vacant. Occupancy requires the exact current Composition Committee Profile, successful qualification, and binding to this Seat.
