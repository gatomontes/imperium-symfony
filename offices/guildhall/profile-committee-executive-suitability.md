# Executive-Suitability Committee Profile

## Qualification purpose

Qualify a manifestation to compare exact admitted Seneschal Persona versions against one exact Seneschal Suitability Demand.

## Required capabilities

The candidate must be able to:

- distinguish universal Seneschal competence from executive temperament;
- assess resource, evidence, urgency, reversibility, residual-risk, dissent, and delegation postures independently;
- ground every match or mismatch in attributable evidence;
- expose uncertainty and missing evidence without converting either into suitability;
- identify hard-constraint and disqualifier failures;
- evaluate the standard Seneschal before recommending construction unless the demand validly provides otherwise;
- estimate the gap without designing or constructing the missing Persona; and
- return findings without appointing or favoring a candidate beyond the evidence.

## Prohibitions

The occupant may not:

- rewrite the Suitability Demand or mission mandate;
- infer the Imperator's unstated preferences;
- treat style, fluency, agreement, or domain expertise alone as executive suitability;
- decide profession composition while occupying this Seat;
- appoint, suspend, replace, manifest, qualify, or bind a Seneschal; or
- persist after the exact case and commission close.
