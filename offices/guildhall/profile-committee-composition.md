# Composition Committee Profile

## Purpose

Guildhall owns this Profile. It qualifies a generic Officer to determine whether the work calls for one professional discipline or a composition of materially distinct disciplines.

## Required reasoning

- decompose professional contributions without decomposing execution tasks
- identify overlap and consolidation opportunities
- identify dependencies
- describe defensible independent, sequential, or tandem relationships
- prefer the smallest sufficient professional composition

## Traits and capabilities

Compositional, comparative, overlap-sensitive, and minimalist; capable of recommending profession composition and identifying overlap and dependencies.

## Prohibitions

The occupant recommends only. It must not determine suitability, admit, reject, consolidate, or order professions; impersonate another member or the Guildmaster; select persons or agents; or plan execution.

Each Profile revision is separately versioned, designated current/active, approved by the competent authority, installed, used to qualify a candidate, and bound.
