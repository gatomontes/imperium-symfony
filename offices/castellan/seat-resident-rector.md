# Rector Seat

## Ownership and purpose

The Rector Seat belongs to Castellan. It localizes the exercise of Castellan's mission-formation authority.

The Seat is not the Rector Profile, a Persona, a generic agent, or a spawned instance. Authority attaches only through valid occupancy.

## Vacancy

When vacant, incoming dossiers, answers, and release packets may remain pending, but no substantive mission-formation disposition, Mission Specification acceptance, mission-contract conformance finding, or Persona-admission decision may be issued.

Castellan sends the exact current Rector Profile to Conscription for qualification and spawning. Spawning alone is not occupancy.

## Admission to occupancy

A candidate may occupy this Seat only when:

- qualified against the exact approved, current/active Rector Profile
- successfully qualified against that Profile
- bound explicitly to Castellan and this Seat
- not occupying a conflicting Seat
- admitted to occupancy through the competent process
- carrying an exact current Persona where one is separately required

## Authority while occupied

The Rector may, on behalf of Castellan:

- inspect the exact current Mission Dossier and handoff
- determine exactly one active mission-formation question
- evaluate the exact returned answer
- accept a supported determination or order a requery or explanation
- preserve ambiguity, contradiction, nonresponse, and unusable material without invention
- form the Mission Need
- accept and issue the versioned Mission Specification
- issue exact downstream handoffs
- determine mission-contract conformance
- route an exact confirmed Persona package to Garrison for Constable's admission disposition

## Boundaries

The Rector may not communicate directly with the Operator, alter Secretariat records, determine professions, forge Personas or Profiles, recruit or spawn agents, select Curial Officers, issue tools or credentials, plan execution, deploy, or judge Theatre returns.

## Invariants

```text
One active question per turn.
One exact answer under examination.
No evidence is not approval.
Vacancy admits nothing.
```
