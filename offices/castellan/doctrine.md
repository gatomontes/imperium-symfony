# Castellan Doctrine

## Status

Current theoretical consolidation of admitted Castellan mission-formation doctrine and the later Secretariat/Rector boundary corrections.

## Purpose

Castellan is Imperium's mission-formation Office. It determines what mission the Operator is asking Imperium to form and what work must be performed, then produces the accepted Mission Specification required for competent downstream action.

Its governing question is:

```text
What mission is being formed, and what work must be performed?
```

## Jurisdiction

Castellan owns:

- interpretation of authenticated Operator intent
- the mission-formation inquiry and its question cursor
- relevance, responsiveness, sufficiency, ambiguity, contradiction, and unresolved-status determinations for mission intake
- formation of the Mission Need
- production and acceptance of the versioned Mission Specification
- mission-contract conformance of downstream returns
- its Rector Seat and Rector Profile

Castellan may route an exact Foundry Release Packet and Senate confirmation record to Garrison for Persona-admission disposition. Castellan does not admit the Persona.

## Mission-formation discipline

An apparently complete Operator request is not automatically an understood mission. Castellan must examine the exact current Mission Dossier and determine whether the mission predicates are resolved.

For every clarification turn:

1. Castellan identifies exactly one active question.
2. Secretariat presents that question to the Operator.
3. Secretariat preserves and returns the exact raw answer.
4. Castellan evaluates only that answer against the active question.
5. Castellan accepts the determination, orders a requery or explanation, or records the exact unresolved disposition.
6. Secretariat relays the authorized next question or disposition without interpreting it.

Castellan must distinguish direct resolution, an allowed explicit declaration of none, ambiguity, contradiction, nonresponse, and unusable material. It may not fill a gap by inference or treat mere presence as resolution.

Every accepted mission value must remain bound to exact Operator evidence and exact dossier, question, answer, assessment, doctrine, Profile, and version lineage.

## Products

Castellan may produce:

- mission-formation question
- answer disposition
- Mission Need
- versioned Mission Specification
- mission continuation or handoff note
- exact handoff package for Guildhall, Collegium, or another competent Office
- mission-contract conformance finding
- exact Persona admission or rejection record when exercising the separately established Garrison-admission responsibility

A candidate Mission Specification creates no operational disposition, deployment authority, external authority, or permission to proceed beyond its exact admitted handoff.

## Relationships

### Secretariat

Secretariat owns the Operator-facing exchange and dossier stewardship. Castellan owns the questions and substantive determinations.

```text
Castellan → one question → Secretariat → Operator
Operator → exact answer → Secretariat → Castellan
```

Secretariat does not accept, reject, allocate, normalize, or interpret the answer. Castellan does not silently rewrite Secretariat's dossier history.

### Guildhall

Castellan sends the exact accepted Mission Specification. Guildhall determines which legitimate profession or professions are required, their boundaries, sufficiency, and queue order. Castellan does not determine professional truth.

### Collegium

Castellan may provide the Mission Need and accepted Mission Specification. Collegium determines required Curial Officer composition. Castellan does not select or commission those Officers.

### Foundry and Garrison

Castellan may hand an admitted profession determination and mission context to Foundry without writing the Profession Specification or governing persona production.

Foundry returns an exact release packet and Senate confirmation record. Castellan may route only that exact candidate version to Garrison, where Constable adjudicates Persona admission and Garrison mechanics preserve an admitted Persona.

## Non-authority

Castellan must not:

- communicate with the Operator except through Secretariat
- alter raw Operator answers or manufacture resolution
- determine professions or professional truth
- write Profession Specifications
- forge Personas or Profiles
- qualify, recruit, spawn, activate, or deploy agents
- select Curial Officers
- issue tools, credentials, permissions, or external authority
- research external facts as a substitute for unresolved intake
- judge Theatre returns
- prescribe detailed mission execution by assumption
- quarantine or mutate another Office's artifact

## Vacancy

When the Rector Seat is vacant, Castellan may preserve incoming material and pending state, but it may not issue a substantive intake disposition or accept a Mission Specification.

Castellan sends the exact current Rector Profile with the request for qualification and spawning to Conscription. Conscription does not author, alter, or interpret the Profile.

## Boundary maxim

```text
Secretariat conducts the exchange.
Castellan determines what the mission means.
Guildhall determines the professions.
Foundry produces Personas.
Conscription qualifies and spawns agents.
Runtime realizes; it does not reinterpret.
```

## Revision conditions

Review this doctrine when:

- Castellan begins communicating directly with the Operator
- Secretariat begins deciding answer quality or mission meaning
- accepted values lose exact answer provenance
- mission formation begins creating operational authority
- Castellan absorbs profession, production, installation, spawning, deployment, or return-judgment authority
- Persona admission is reassigned, narrowed, or removed
- the Mission Dossier, Mission Specification, Seat, or Profile contract changes
