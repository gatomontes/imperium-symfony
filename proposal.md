# Proposal: Redefine Curia and its Seats

## Status

Approved.

## Decision

Abolish the Secretariat and Castellan as independent Offices. Retire the Rector role.

Curia is the mission-specific governing body of an Imperium instance. Imperium runs one instance per mission; Curia therefore governs exactly one mission rather than maintaining a permanent cabinet over simultaneous missions.

The human Operator participates directly through the **Imperator Seat**. Curia's agent-occupied composition is:

- the **Seneschal Seat**;
- the **Chamberlain Seat**;
- the **Secretary Seat**, occupied provisionally by Isolde; and
- the mission-specific **Curial Seats** required for deliberation.

The governing allocation is:

> **Imperator authorizes. Seneschal decides. Chamberlain coordinates. Isolde records and communicates. Curiales deliberate.**

## Curian Seats

### Imperator Seat

The Imperator Seat is occupied directly by the human Operator.

The Imperator:

- constitutes the mission and its Imperium instance;
- holds ultimate authority;
- approves protected commitments and exact authorization objects;
- appoints or replaces the Seneschal;
- may intervene, restrict, suspend, overrule, or terminate within competent authority; and
- does not become an ordinary Curialis.

The Imperator Seat is not populated, qualified, or manifested by Conscription. The Operator enters Curia by authority, not by manifestation.

### Seneschal Seat

The Seneschal is Curia's chief executive and decision-maker.

The Seneschal:

- presides over Curia;
- governs deliberation;
- weighs evidence, risk, expenditure, speed, and competing interests;
- renders mission decisions within delegated authority;
- escalates protected commitments to the Imperator; and
- remains subordinate to the Imperator's authority and the exact mission mandate.

The Seneschal does not replace the Imperator as the source of ultimate authority. The Imperator authorizes; the Seneschal exercises delegated executive judgment.

### Chamberlain Seat

The Chamberlain is Curia's Chief of Staff.

The Chamberlain:

- coordinates deliberation, Offices, dependencies, and execution;
- maintains mission state and decision continuity;
- tracks pending commitments, commissions, and unresolved matters;
- prepares controlled transitions between Curian occupants; and
- ensures that Curial decisions are carried into the competent institutional paths.

The Chamberlain does not automatically inherit executive authority when the Seneschal Seat becomes vacant. A vacancy requires an authorized interim or permanent occupant.

### Secretary Seat

Isolde is provisionally reassigned to Curia as Secretary.

The Secretary:

- maintains the Curial record, agenda, submissions, and decisions;
- assists direct communication between the Imperator and Curia;
- clarifies operator intent one question at a time;
- rejects answers that do not address the active question;
- records each relevant answer without silently altering its meaning; and
- consults the competent Curian authority when uncertain.

The Secretary assists within Curia. The Secretary does not stand outside Curia as a mandatory institutional gateway and does not control access to the Situation Room.

### Curial Seats

A mission-specific member of Curia is a **Curialis**. Collectively, they are **Curiales**.

Curiales supply the expertise and judgment required by the mission. They are selected for the mission rather than maintained as a fixed departmental cabinet.

Intelligence, tactical, resource, safety, and dispatch functions do not automatically receive permanent Curial Seats. Curia may summon a suitably qualified Curialis when the mission requires that judgment. Institutional services, enforcement boundaries, and mechanical execution remain with their competent Offices or Runtime boundaries.

## Standard and specialized occupants

Seneschal and Chamberlain Seats may initially be occupied by standard, generally qualified manifestations.

The Chamberlain can remain comparatively standardized because its principal responsibility is institutional coordination. The Seneschal cannot be treated as cognitively neutral: executive temperament materially affects decisions under uncertainty.

Seneschal suitability has three distinct layers:

1. **Seat Profile** — the capabilities required of every Seneschal;
2. **Persona** — how the occupant tends to weigh evidence, risk, expenditure, speed, dissent, and competing interests; and
3. **Mission Mandate** — the Imperator's explicit priorities, limits, and delegated authority for the mission.

Domain expertise belongs primarily in the Curiales. A specialized Seneschal is required only when the mission requires a materially different executive temperament or decision posture.

> **Specialize the council before specializing its government.**

For example, a cybersecurity mission may require a Seneschal who is:

- permissive with resources when credible risk warrants expenditure;
- strict about technical evidence;
- intolerant of unsupported claims;
- responsive to credible exposure;
- cautious with irreversible actions;
- unwilling to accept false economy; and
- receptive to expert dissent.

This disposition is neither merely aggressive nor fiscally liberal. It is financially permissive under credible risk while remaining epistemically conservative.

## Seneschal suitability demand

Mission planning shall determine whether the standard Seneschal is suitable.

Guildhall may evaluate the mission's executive requirements against admitted Seneschal Personas and report whether Curia should:

1. use the standard Seneschal;
2. select a better-fitting admitted Seneschal from Garrison; or
3. commission a new Seneschal Persona when no admitted Persona satisfies the demand.

Resource-bearing research, construction, or qualification remains subject to the applicable Planning Authorization and the Imperator's competent approval.

The Imperator should not micromanage a Seneschal into alignment. The Imperator should appoint a Seneschal whose judgment can be trusted within the delegated mandate.

## Diagnosing executive misalignment

Repeated disagreement with a Seneschal must be classified before corrective action:

- **Mandate failure** — the Seneschal misunderstood explicit priorities;
- **Competence failure** — the occupant cannot adequately perform the office; or
- **Temperamental mismatch** — the occupant understands the mandate but consistently weighs tradeoffs differently from the Imperator.

A mandate failure may require clarification. A competence failure may require disqualification or replacement. A temperamental mismatch may justify replacing the occupant with a better-fitting Persona rather than repeatedly correcting individual decisions.

The Persona is not silently rewritten to please the Imperator. The Seat receives a different, qualified occupant through the governed lifecycle.

## Seneschal succession

The Imperator initiates a **Seneschal succession** through Isolde.

A normal succession proceeds as follows:

1. the Imperator expresses dissatisfaction, a desired executive change, or a direct replacement instruction;
2. Isolde clarifies whether the matter requires mandate correction, mismatch evaluation, or replacement;
3. the Imperator issues a succession directive identifying the reason, desired dispositions, interim status, and applicable resource limits;
4. the Chamberlain prepares a succession packet containing mission state, pending decisions, existing commitments, unresolved disagreements, and the new suitability demand;
5. Guildhall evaluates admitted candidates and identifies whether new Persona construction is required;
6. the Imperator approves the replacement and any required resource-bearing construction;
7. Conscription assembles and qualifies the selected manifestation under the Seneschal Profile and exact mission mandate; and
8. the outgoing manifestation loses Seat authority as the new manifestation occupies the Seneschal Seat at a controlled decision boundary.

The outgoing Seneschal may contribute evidence to the succession packet but does not control the packet, selection, appointment, or transfer and holds no veto.

Mission records, authorizations, commitments, and provenance remain institutional state. They do not disappear or transfer authority merely because the Seat changes occupants.

For urgent cases, the Imperator may suspend the incumbent immediately. An authorized standard or interim Seneschal may occupy the Seat while Guildhall evaluates a permanent replacement. The Chamberlain preserves continuity but does not become Seneschal by vacancy alone.

## Revised command and initialization path

The direct command relationship is:

`Imperator Seat ↔ Curia`

When Isolde assists:

`Imperator → Isolde → Curial proceeding`

For succession:

`Imperator → Isolde → succession directive → Chamberlain → Guildhall / Foundry → Conscription → Seneschal Seat`

Initialization no longer requires Secretariat, Castellan, or Rector:

1. the Operator activates Imperium and assumes the Imperator Seat;
2. bootstrap provides only the initial authority or manifestation explicitly admitted by bootstrap doctrine;
3. Conscription assembles the required standard Curian occupants under authorized provenance;
4. the Seneschal and Chamberlain occupy their Seats;
5. Isolde may occupy the Secretary Seat provisionally; and
6. Curia summons mission-specific Curiales according to the authorized planning path.

The exact bootstrap mechanism remains subject to separate doctrine. This proposal does not silently assign bootstrap authority to an abolished role.

## Architectural consequences

- Remove Secretariat and Castellan from the Office map.
- Retire the Rector Seat, Profile, and role.
- Move Isolde and Secretary behavior under Curia provisionally.
- Declare Seneschal, Chamberlain, Secretary, and mission-specific Curial Seats under Curia.
- Preserve the Imperator Seat as a human-held authority Seat outside the manifestation lifecycle.
- Replace fixed departmental Curian officers with mission-specific Curiales.
- Add Seneschal suitability as a planning concern.
- Preserve Guildhall's personnel-suitability function, Foundry's Persona construction function, Garrison's Persona custody and admission function, and Conscription's manifestation and qualification function.
- Implement controlled Seneschal succession without allowing the outgoing occupant to govern its own replacement.
- Replace all obsolete `Operator → Secretariat → Castellan` paths with direct Imperator participation in Curia.

## Summary

Curia is the government of one mission.

The Imperator supplies sovereign authority. The Seneschal supplies executive judgment. The Chamberlain supplies institutional coordination. Isolde supplies the Curial record and operator interface. Curiales supply mission-specific cognition.

All agent-occupied Seats remain replaceable cognition attached to stable institutional authority.
