# Delegate Mission Personnel-Use Decision

The Imperator decides one exact Step 4 identity-bearing Delegate personnel-use request using `AUTHORIZED`, `REFUSED`, `RETURNED_FOR_REVISION`, `ALTERNATIVE_PROPOSED`, `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, or `DEFERRED`.

Only `AUTHORIZED` creates one exact, single-use, Guildmaster-held personnel-use authority bound to the unchanged capability correlation, profession, Persona identity and custody facts, suitability record, Mission Plan lineage, and explicit Imperator limitations. Authorization requires non-empty limitations.

Authorization stops at:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_PERSONNEL_USE_AUTHORIZED_PENDING_GUILDHALL_ACCEPTANCE`

Every other disposition stops at:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_NON_AUTHORIZING_IMPERATOR_PERSONNEL_USE_DISPOSITION_RECORDED`

No disposition reserves or retrieves the Persona. Even authorization grants no Guildhall acceptance, reservation, custody transfer, Profile lifecycle, Manifestation assembly, Seat binding, deployment, operational use, cognition, provider, data, tool, credential, perimeter, external-action, execution, or continuing-turn authority.
