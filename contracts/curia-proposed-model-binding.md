# Curia proposed model binding

Curia consumes one recommendation-bound selection-decision authority to issue `SELECT_RECOMMENDED_MODEL`, `SELECT_OTHER_ELIGIBLE_MODEL`, `REJECT_ALL_MODELS`, or `RETURN_NEW_COMMISSION`. A selected model must be one exact eligible provider/model/version from the sealed Oracle recommendation. Departure from Augur's recommendation requires an explicit attributable rationale and preserves objections and deliberation provenance.

A selection made here is planning evidence only. It creates a `PROPOSED_MODEL_BINDING_PENDING_PLAN_AUTHORIZATION` for inclusion in the complete versioned planning dossier. It does not create an isolated approval request, operational binding, model assignment, Profile mutation, credential release, provider invocation, deployment, or execution authority.

Imperator reviews the complete dossier and may approve it, object to exact terms, or return it for revision. Only valid approval of the exact dossier version may authorize later binding work.
