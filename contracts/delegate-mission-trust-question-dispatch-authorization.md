# Delegate mission trust-question dispatch authorization

Step 22 permits the current exact Lord Speaker to authorize or refuse dispatch of the sealed Step 21 trust question. The Lord Speaker revalidates the authored question, proceeding security, and the same current Bailiff recorded at Stand admission.

Authorization opens one single-use, identity-bound Bailiff authority to dispatch the exact question unchanged. Refusal opens nothing. Neither branch dispatches the question, invokes the examination subject, receives testimony, or grants findings or operational authority.
