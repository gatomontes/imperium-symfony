# Oracle model comparative assessment

Augur may consume a completed eligibility phase's single comparative-assessment authority to seal one normalized, criterion-by-criterion matrix covering every and only `ELIGIBLE` exact model version. Each matrix cell cites evidence already bound to that model's eligibility finding and records its observation, evidence strength, limitations, and unresolved contradictions.

The assessment may expose differences but may not assign an aggregate score, ordinal position, winner, preferred candidate, or selection. Sealing consumes comparative-assessment authority and opens exactly one single-use recommendation authority bound to the unchanged commission, candidate set, findings, and matrix digest.

The normal checkpoint is `ORACLE_COMPARATIVE_ASSESSMENT_SEALED_PENDING_AUGUR_RECOMMENDATION`. It grants no selection, assignment, Profile mutation, provider invocation, deployment, or execution authority.
