# Delegate mission Profile-approval decision

Step 43 permits Imperator to independently decide the exact sealed Senate disposition and Delegate Profile candidate as `APPROVED`, `REFUSED`, `RETURNED_FOR_REVISION`, `ALTERNATIVE_PROPOSED`, `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, or `DEFERRED`.

Imperator may approve only when Senate disposition is `APPROVED` and no mandatory Security blocking condition exists. Approval opens one exact single-use request to Conscription for operational qualification. It does not qualify or install the Profile, assemble a Manifestation, bind the mission Seat, transfer custody, deploy, use resources, invoke a provider, act externally, or execute.
