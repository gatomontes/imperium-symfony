# Oracle canonical catalogue snapshot

Oracle may construct its catalogue only from previously admitted, sealed, digest-valid model evidence and exact provider-access assertions persisted by Clavium through Locksmith. Augur receives references and classifications; it does not receive credentials or conduct external research in this operation.

Knowledge, accessibility, and admissibility remain independent. A Clavium assertion is mandatory for `ACCESSIBLE`, `RESTRICTED`, or `UNAVAILABLE`; `UNVERIFIED` may explicitly record the absence of an assertion. The assertion's provider, instance, status, provenance, and digest must agree with the admitted evidence and resulting classification.

The immutable terminal boundary is `ORACLE_CANONICAL_CATALOGUE_SNAPSHOT_SEALED_NO_SELECTION_AUTHORITY`. It grants no research, requirement commission, eligibility, recommendation, selection, assignment, Profile mutation, credential disclosure, provider invocation, deployment, or execution authority.
