# Continuous-governance internal reconstruction

## Version

`imperium.continuous-governance-internal-reconstruction/v1`

## Purpose

Provide one read-only, fail-closed reconstruction of a completed internal governance-cognition
provider iteration from native Imperium artifacts. The view starts from one durable governance
invocation claim and follows exact stored references backward and forward without replaying
the provider or persisting a new judgment.

## Included evidence

The reconstruction verifies exactly seven artifacts:

1. the Batch 2 governance-event envelope;
2. the classified native governance-cognition request;
3. the independent Imperator provider-resource decision;
4. the opaque Clavium governance-cognition lease;
5. the durable lease-and-native-authority invocation claim;
6. the mutable provider invocation journal at sealed-response identity; and
7. the immutable provider response envelope.

Every artifact must be digest-intact. Request, decision, lease, claim, event, journal, and
response references must agree on their exact identities, digests, instance, cluster, target,
input, provider/model lineage, and response identity where applicable. Missing or substituted
evidence fails stopped.

## Mechanical exclusions

The result must report that it does not reconstruct the underlying cluster-native authority
record, Institutional Decision Integrity bundle, Delegate pre-deployment governance,
deployment, Persona custody, terminal retirement, native post-provider result, external effect,
telemetry, revocation, containment, or incident evidence. Those exclusions cannot be inferred
as validated merely because this seven-artifact subchain is valid.

## Authority boundary

Reconstruction is read-only. It grants and consumes no cognition, provider, credential, tool,
network, perimeter, external-action, execution, continuation, revocation, containment, or
incident authority and cannot repair or manufacture missing evidence.
