# Delegate Mission Guildhall Intake and Personnel Resolution

## Step 2 — intake disposition

Only the exact active Office-bound Guildmaster, explicitly classified as `LEGATE`, may accept or refuse one sealed Step 1 Delegate capability demand. Refusal closes that exact demand without personnel authority. Acceptance opens one single-use authority limited to profession determination and Persona suitability determination against authoritative Garrison facts.

Acceptance is not profession determination, Persona selection, suitability, personnel-use authorization, reservation, retrieval, custody transfer, Profile work, assembly, Seat binding, deployment, cognition, resource use, external action, or execution.

## Step 3 — profession and Persona suitability resolution

The same exact Guildmaster consumes the Step 2 authority and binds one profession determination and one `SUITABLE`, `NO_SUITABLE_PERSONA`, or `UNRESOLVED` disposition to the unchanged capability demand and exact authoritative Garrison inventory response.

Garrison supplies custody and availability facts but neither ranks nor selects. A `SUITABLE` Persona must be an exact `ADMITTED_HELD`, available custody record contained in that response. Guildhall may not substitute an unreported Persona.

Only `SUITABLE` opens a single-use authority to present the exact identity-bearing personnel-use request to Curia. This is request authority, not personnel-use authority. No Step 3 branch reserves, retrieves, transfers custody, derives a Profile, assembles a Manifestation, binds a Seat, deploys, invokes cognition, uses resources, crosses the perimeter, acts externally, or executes.
