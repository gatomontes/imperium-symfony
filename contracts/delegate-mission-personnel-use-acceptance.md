# Delegate Mission Personnel-Use Acceptance and Reservation Request

Only the same exact active Office-bound Guildmaster may accept an `AUTHORIZED` Step 5 Delegate personnel-use act. Runtime revalidates the exact Step 4 request, Step 3 resolution, unchanged personnel commitment, Imperator limitations, single-use personnel-use authority, and current Guildmaster binding.

Acceptance consumes only the Step 5 personnel-use authority and issues one exact request to `garrison.constable`:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_PERSONA_RESERVATION_REQUESTED_PENDING_CONSTABLE_DISPOSITION`

The combined Step 6 checkpoint is:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_PERSONNEL_USE_AUTHORIZATION_ACCEPTED_RESERVATION_REQUESTED_PENDING_CONSTABLE_DISPOSITION`

Guildhall does not reserve, retrieve, substitute, or transfer custody of the Persona. The request grants no reservation, Profile, assembly, Seat-binding, deployment, cognition, provider, tool, credential, perimeter, external-action, execution, or continuing-turn authority. A non-authorizing Imperator disposition or changed Guildmaster cannot be accepted.
