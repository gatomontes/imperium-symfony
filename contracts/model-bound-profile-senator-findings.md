# Independent model-bound Profile Senator findings

Trust, Security, and Usability each consume only their own finding authority against their own sealed evidence turn. Findings remain mutually invisible during authorship and must declare disposition, severity, evidence, rationale, limitations, uncertainty, and rubric-bound defect attribution.

Permitted dispositions are PASS, CONCERN, FAIL, and UNRESOLVED. Permitted severities are NONE, LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, and CRITICAL. PASS requires NONE and no defect. Every non-pass finding requires an attributed defect.

A Security FAIL, or a Security UNRESOLVED finding at HIGH or CRITICAL severity, seals a mandatory blocking condition. It cannot be averaged away.
