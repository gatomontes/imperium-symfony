# Delegate mission deployment and custody transition

Step 47 assigns the operational deployment decision to the exact occupied Seneschal. Authorization binds only the already-sealed mission use and opens one single-use Constable custody-transition authority. Refusal, return, and deferral open nothing.

Step 48 permits the exact occupied Constable to move custody from `ADMITTED_HELD` to `DELEGATE_MISSION_DEPLOYED_BOUND`. Deployment and custody transition do not activate the Delegate, invoke cognition, release resources, cross a perimeter, act externally, or execute.
