# Delegate mission first-question commission disposition

Step 20 permits only the exact occupied trust Senator named by the Step 19 commission to consume its single-use acceptance-or-refusal authority.

Acceptance binds the Senator to the unchanged trust jurisdiction, hearing contract, evidence rules, and one-question limit. It opens one single-use question-authorship authority for that exact binding. Dispatch is expressly excluded.

Refusal opens no question authority. Neither disposition authors or dispatches a question, invokes cognition, opens testimony or findings, approves or installs a Profile, or grants operational or execution authority.
