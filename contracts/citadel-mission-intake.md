## Purpose and ownership

Citadel receives requests across its Curia instances, discusses them with the
Imperator, checks existing work, prepares proposals when authorized, and arranges
handoff of approved missions to new mission-specific Curiae. Citadel coordinates
the collection; each Curia governs its own assigned mission.

A request is not a mission and does not instantiate a provisional Curia. Existing
mission inquiries route to their existing Curia. A new Curia is arranged only
after approval of the exact mission proposal and through the competent constitution,
appointment and authority-derivation paths. Approval does not itself instantiate
officers, deploy them or execute the mission.

Each Curia has its own cognitive Secretary function, normally Isolde, supporting
its Seneschal and mission record. That Secretary is not the shared front door.
No separate Secretariat Office or additional executive approval layer is created
by this decision. Existing direct authenticated access remains available when
clerical support is absent.

Citadel's interview, relevance judgment and proposal preparation belong to
Castellan, its owner-selected cognitive officer. Implementation must establish
his Seat, qualification, mandate and actual cognition binding; an orchestration
service cannot pretend to make those judgments. Assignment alone does not qualify,
appoint or activate a manifestation. Do not
automatically borrow a mission's Seneschal, invent a new Legate, or treat a
mechanical appointment as selection, qualification or a source of authority.

## Default sequence

1. **Receive and route.** Preserve the exact request at Citadel. Identify existing
   mission inquiries without creating another mission. Ambiguity is discussed
   with the Imperator. Intake persistence is distinct from metered cognition.
2. **Understand the request and compare existing work.** The competent Citadel
   cognitive officer conducts the conversation directly with the Imperator.
   Registry facts inform comparison with pending, active and relevant completed
   work. Questions and challenges serve understanding of intent, outcome and
   constraints. Consult another Seneschal only when existing records cannot
   resolve a material question; no mandatory consultation of every Curia.
3. **Declare understanding.** The interview's sole completion criterion is the
   responsible cognitive officer's attributable “I understand.” Preserve its
   understood intent, exact exchange and objections. Understanding is not
   agreement, endorsement, feasibility, proposal readiness or permission to draft.
4. **Request permission to draft.** Separately state readiness and present the
   concrete drafting scope, resources, disclosures, limits and expiry before asking:
   “I understand. I am ready to draft a proposal. Do you approve?”
   The understanding declaration may be accompanied by this request, never by an
   already elaborated proposal. The short question does not conceal material terms.
5. **Record the Imperator's drafting decision.** Only valid affirmative approval
   of the exact disclosed drafting request permits actual proposal elaboration
   within its bounds. The drafting permission uses the applicable planning
   authority machinery; it is not approval of the eventual mission. Decline or
   deferral preserves the request and closes or suspends its applicable resources.
   Returning later preserves information but does not revive expired authority.
6. **Draft and present at Citadel.** After drafting approval, Citadel prepares
   the actual proposal and numbered dossier and presents it to the Imperator.
   Investigation, other Offices, protected data or external actions are permitted
   only where expressly covered by valid planning authority. Otherwise disclose
   the additional requirement before using it. Revisions preserve old versions;
   material changes require the applicable new decision.
7. **Approve the mission.** The Imperator separately decides the exact proposal.
   Drafting approval never substitutes for Mission Authorization. Recheck relevant
   overlap and competing commitments before admitting new mission work.
8. **Arrange the new Curia and hand off.** Through competent authority, establish
   the mission's Curia and its officers. Deliver the approved plan/version,
   authority references, original exchange, understood intent, rationale,
   objections, unresolved questions, overlap disposition and relevant evidence.
   Retain canonical Citadel records and bind the Curia's mission copy to them.
9. **Accept the handoff and govern the mission.** The receiving Seneschal assesses
   understanding and responsibility under the exact mandate. Acceptance is not
   a second Imperator approval or automatic permission to execute. Clarify genuine
   gaps and contest incompatibilities without automatically repeating the initial
   interview or reopening unchanged terms. A material amendment follows competent
   approval; historical approval cannot authorize changed scope. Only valid
   downstream commissions and execution prerequisites permit work.

These steps describe institutional order, not invented commands or schema names.
They occur by default without requiring governance wording in the mission request.

## Overlap and concurrency

Citadel owns one shared intake/mission coordination record with registered
child-Curia roots and distinct intake, mission and Curia identities. A child Curia
does not found another Imperium. Registry retrieval,
version checks, reservations and admission are mechanical; semantic similarity,
relevance and the recommendation are cognitive. Similar work may be a legitimate
independent review. Present consequential alternatives to the Imperator: reuse,
join, amend, defer or deliberately commission separate work.

Do not auto-reject on a similarity score or let two simultaneous “nothing found”
checks authorize duplicate commitments. Implementation must provide atomic admission
or reservation against exact registry versions, with contention, expiry and
recheck semantics. Pending requests must be visible where relevant. Registry
visibility itself is scoped by lawful access and is not permission to expose
all other missions' source material.

## Resource and authority boundaries

Interview cognition requires legitimate authority before it occurs. The later
drafting decision cannot authorize an interview retroactively or depend on an
already completed proposal. The selected policy is a bounded interview-session
grant: one authentic decision covers disclosed interview scope and resources,
with exact per-call validation and aggregate accounting. Reserve maximum exposure
atomically before I/O; enforce limits and retain reservations on unknown outcomes.
Within unchanged bounds, do not ask for approval before each question. Expiry,
revocation, exhaustion or material scope change stops for amendment. Drafting is
not covered by interview funding. No live budget, tariff, model or spend is
selected here. See the decision record for required disclosure and enforcement.

“I understand” and “ready to draft” are separate cognitive claims. Neither creates
authority. Drafting approval, proposal approval, appointment, Curia handoff
acceptance and execution remain separate events; do not add repeated approvals
when an unchanged action is already covered by valid authority.

No new Curia may be created as a hidden interview prerequisite. Mechanical
appointment can apply only an established rule under competent authority to an
eligible officer. No ready-made Profile, fixture or temporary founding exception
may be fabricated to fill a missing production connection.

Mission constitution and exact appointments may share the mission-approval
presentation when candidates, eligibility and terms are known, while retaining
distinct typed authority effects. If unresolved, return only for that specific
boundary. No standing automatic appointment policy is inferred. Full handoff and
Seneschal acceptance preserve source Citadel and target Curia authority scopes.

## Migration and proof

This allocation supersedes the initial mission-bound Seneschal interview assignment
and the assumption that understanding alone opens drafting. Existing Curian
request/respond, supplied-plan and source-review paths remain source evidence;
their names do not establish compliance with Citadel-led intake.

Preserve completed missions, the old Batch 0 evidence, current installation and
sealed bootstrap artifacts. Future source amendments do not automatically amend
active Profiles or create operational authority. Audit remaining contracts,
issuer/consumer assumptions and instance identity boundaries before migration.

Prove: intake without Curia creation; lawful cognitive authorship; understanding
with dissent; zero actual drafting before the distinct drafting approval; exact
version and limit enforcement; refusal/deferral/revision; overlap contention; and
approved, attributable handoff to a mission Curia with no execution-authority leak.
No live capability is established by documentation or isolated fake-provider tests.
