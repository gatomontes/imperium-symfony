# Delegate mission Profile-scope decision

Step 9 records Imperator's disposition on the exact immutable Step 8 request.

Allowed dispositions are `AUTHORIZED`, `REFUSED`, `RETURNED_FOR_REVISION`, `ALTERNATIVE_PROPOSED`, `CLARIFICATION_REQUIRED`, and `DEFERRED`. Only `AUTHORIZED` opens one single-use authority held by `conscription.recruiter` to accept and commission derivation of one Profile from the exact sealed scope.

Authorization stops at `DELEGATE_MISSION_PROFILE_DERIVATION_AUTHORIZED_PENDING_CONSCRIPTION_ACCEPTANCE`. It does not derive, instantiate, activate, examine, approve, install, assemble, bind, deploy, or operate a Delegate.

Every non-authorizing disposition grants no authority.
