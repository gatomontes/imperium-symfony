# Delegate mission Profile-derivation commission request

Step 10 lets the occupied Conscription Recruiter accept or refuse the exact authorized Step 9 scope.

Acceptance consumes the single-use Imperator authority and issues one custody-bound request to `laboratorium.alchemist`. The Persona remains `ADMITTED_HELD` by Garrison; the commission conveys no custody-transfer or substitution authority.

The commission stops at `DELEGATE_MISSION_PROFILE_DERIVATION_COMMISSION_REQUESTED_PENDING_ALCHEMIST_ACCEPTANCE`. It opens one single-use acceptance-disposition authority for the occupied Alchemist. Its derivation authority is present but non-exercisable until Laboratorium independently accepts the exact request.

Refusal issues no commission and grants no authority.
