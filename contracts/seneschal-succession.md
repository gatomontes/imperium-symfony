# Seneschal Succession Contract

## Purpose

This contract governs correction, evaluation, suspension, and replacement of the Seneschal without allowing the incumbent to control succession or allowing mission state to become occupant property.

## Distinct dispositions

An Imperator instruction must be classified as exactly one of:

- `clarify_mandate` — retain the incumbent and issue a new exact mandate version;
- `evaluate_mismatch` — preserve current authority while authorized evaluation determines whether mismatch exists;
- `replace` — begin ordinary succession under declared interim conditions; or
- `suspend_and_replace` — remove executive authority immediately and begin replacement.

Correction of a decision is not automatically replacement. Repeated disagreement must remain classifiable as mandate failure, competence failure, or temperamental mismatch.

## Succession Directive

The authenticated Imperator issues an immutable directive identifying:

- mission instance;
- exact incumbent manifestation, Seat, and occupancy generation;
- disposition and stated basis;
- exact current mandate and requested successor demand when applicable;
- incumbent status during succession;
- resource, cost, and time limits;
- applicable authorization and delegation path;
- issue, expiry, revocation, and replay controls; and
- content digest and signature evidence.

Isolde may record and present the instruction. Curia must also accept a direct authenticated Imperator directive when the Secretary Seat is vacant. Recording does not create or enlarge the Imperator's authority.

## Succession Packet

The Chamberlain prepares and seals the succession packet. It contains exact references to:

- the directive and Suitability Demand;
- mission dossier and current mandate;
- active Planning and Mission Authorizations;
- pending decisions and unresolved disagreements;
- active commitments and commissions;
- dependencies, stop conditions, and material deadlines;
- evidence and provenance indexes;
- any attributable incumbent contribution; and
- expected current and next occupancy generations.

The outgoing Seneschal may contribute evidence. It may not edit the Chamberlain's record, remove unfavorable material, select candidates, approve the packet, delay a mandatory suspension, or veto transfer.

## Selection and construction

Guildhall evaluates admitted candidates under the exact Suitability Demand. Garrison supplies inventory facts and the selected admitted Persona version. Foundry constructs a missing Persona only under the applicable authorization. Laboratorium derives the exact Seneschal Profile artifact. Conscription assembles and qualifies the candidate.

None of those acts appoints the candidate or grants Seat authority.

## Seat transfer

MasterMason may execute the exact authorized transfer only when:

- the directive and succession packet are valid, current, unexpired, and mutually consistent;
- incumbent identity and occupancy generation match authoritative state;
- the incoming manifestation is distinct, qualified, and bound to the exact approved Profile, Persona, mandate, and mission instance;
- the appointment is attributable to competent Imperator authority;
- the expected new occupancy generation is unclaimed; and
- no competing succession or Seat reservation exists.

Ordinary replacement must remove the outgoing manifestation's Seat authority before or atomically with binding the successor. Emergency suspension removes authority immediately and may leave the Seat vacant until an interim or permanent successor is validly bound.

At no point may two manifestations hold active Seneschal authority for the same mission instance and occupancy generation.

## Interim Seneschal

An interim occupant requires its own exact appointment, qualified manifestation, mandate, expiry, and Seat binding. The standard Seneschal may serve as interim when suitable. The Chamberlain never becomes interim Seneschal merely by vacancy.

## Completion

Succession completes only after Runtime verifies:

- outgoing authority removed;
- new occupancy generation active or the Seat authoritatively vacant;
- mission state and authorizations preserved without transfer of incumbent authority;
- stale routes and capabilities fenced;
- consumed commissions closed; and
- complete receipts and lineage recorded.

## Failure and replay

Stale identity, generation conflict, invalid qualification, expired authority, packet mismatch, changed mandate, replay, or partial effect halts at the smallest dependent boundary. Recovery proceeds forward from durable receipts. A failed candidate identity, commission, or transfer transaction is never silently reused.
