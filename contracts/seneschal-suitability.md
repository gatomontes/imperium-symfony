# Seneschal Suitability Contract

## Purpose

This contract defines the mission-specific executive disposition required of the Seneschal Seat without confusing Persona, Profile, mandate, or domain expertise.

The Seneschal Profile defines what every Seneschal must be capable of doing. A Seneschal Persona defines how one admitted cognitive identity tends to weigh competing considerations. The mission mandate defines what the Imperator requires and delegates for one mission instance.

```text
Seat Profile ≠ Persona ≠ Mission Mandate ≠ Suitability Demand
```

## Default rule

The standard Seneschal is the default initial occupant and diagnostic baseline. Domain specialization belongs primarily in Curiales.

> **Specialize the council before specializing its government.**

A specialized Seneschal is justified only when the mission requires a materially different executive disposition, not merely because the mission has a specialized subject.

## Suitability Demand

A Suitability Demand is immutable and versioned. It must identify:

- the mission instance and exact mission-mandate version;
- the authoring Curial proceeding and Seneschal disposition;
- the required posture for each applicable executive axis;
- the rationale and evidence references supporting each posture;
- hard constraints, preferred traits, and explicit disqualifiers;
- whether the standard Seneschal must be evaluated first;
- the applicable Planning Authorization when Guildhall evaluation consumes resources; and
- its lineage, digest method, and content digest.

The required executive axes are:

- resource expenditure;
- technical evidence;
- response urgency;
- irreversible action;
- residual risk;
- expert dissent; and
- delegation style.

An axis may be marked `not_material` only with an attributable rationale. Labels do not carry authority outside the exact demand.

## Guildhall disposition

Guildhall compares the exact demand against exact Garrison inventory facts. It returns one of:

1. `standard_suitable`;
2. `admitted_candidate_suitable`;
3. `construction_required`; or
4. `unresolved`.

Guildhall must identify evaluated Persona versions, evidence, material mismatches, uncertainty, estimated construction cost and dependencies where applicable, and the conditions that would revise its finding.

Guildhall does not appoint the Seneschal, admit a Persona, construct a Persona, qualify a manifestation, bind a Seat, or authorize resources.

## Authorization boundary

Curia may formulate a demand from material already lawfully present in the planning proceeding. Commissioning Guildhall, querying protected inventory, conducting research, examining candidates, or constructing a Persona requires the applicable exact authorization and derived commissions.

Suitability does not create appointment authority. The Imperator appoints or replaces the Seneschal through an exact succession or initial-appointment disposition.

## Failure rule

Missing evidence, an omitted material axis, a stale mandate, a changed mission instance, an unavailable Persona, or an unresolved mismatch does not become suitability. The dependent appointment or replacement path fails closed.
