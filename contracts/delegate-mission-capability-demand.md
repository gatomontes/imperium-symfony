# Delegate Mission Capability Demand

Curia may seal one mission-bound Delegate capability demand only from one exact `imperium.mission-authorization/v1` record whose approved planning dossier, exact Mission Plan, Imperator approval, derivation authority, and digests remain valid and unchanged.

The demand carries functional capabilities, expected outcomes, the intended mission Seat, bounded duration, data/tool/credential/perimeter requirements, stop conditions, and the complete return, Seat-unbinding, custody-restoration, and retirement design. It explicitly classifies the prospective Officer as `DELEGATE`.

Curia does not select a profession or Persona. Structured profession or Persona selection fields invalidate the source Mission Plan for this transition. `guildhall.guildmaster` is only the named later consumer; Step 1 neither delivers the demand nor grants Guildhall intake or translation authority.

The terminal checkpoint for this transition is:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_CAPABILITY_DEMAND_SEALED_PENDING_GUILDHALL_INTAKE_NO_PERSONNEL_AUTHORITY`

The seal grants no Mission Plan amendment, delivery, intake, profession translation or determination, Persona selection or suitability, personnel use, reservation, retrieval, custody transfer, Profile lifecycle, assembly, Seat binding, commissioning, follow-up commissioning, deployment, operational use, cognition, provider invocation, data, tool, credential, perimeter, external-action, execution, return-execution, or continuing-turn authority.
