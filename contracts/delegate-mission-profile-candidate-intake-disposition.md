# Delegate mission Profile-candidate intake disposition

Step 13 permits only the current occupied Conscription Recruiter to accept or refuse the exact Step 12 returned candidate.

The Recruiter revalidates the sealed candidate, derivation disposition, immutable scope, Persona, custody lease, and unchanged live `ADMITTED_HELD` Garrison custody. The single-use return-intake authority is consumed by either disposition.

Acceptance opens only one single-use examination-preparation authority. It does not deliver to Senate, authorize examination, approve or install the Profile, or grant any operational authority. Refusal opens nothing.
