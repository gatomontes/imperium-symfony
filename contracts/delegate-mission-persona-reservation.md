# Delegate Mission Persona Reservation Disposition

Only the exact active Office-bound Constable may decide one Step 6 Delegate reservation request. Runtime revalidates the request, Guildhall acceptance, authorized Imperator act and limitations, exact personnel commitment, current Persona identity, live Garrison custody record, availability, instance, and reservation ledgers across Delegate and legacy personnel routes.

Permitted factual dispositions are `RESERVED`, `PERSONA_NOT_ADMITTED`, `PERSONA_UNAVAILABLE`, `PERSONA_ALREADY_RESERVED`, and `DISPOSITION_MISMATCH`. Garrison does not select or propose a substitute.

Success stops at:

`DELEGATE_MISSION_PERSONA_RESERVED_PENDING_PROFILE_SCOPE_CONSTRUCTION`

Reservation retains the Persona in Garrison `ADMITTED_HELD` custody and opens only one exact, single-use Curia authority to construct the immutable Delegate mission Profile-scope request. This is not Profile derivation authority.

Every refusal branch grants nothing. No branch authorizes retrieval, custody transfer, Profile derivation or examination, Manifestation assembly, Seat binding, deployment, operational use, cognition, provider invocation, data/tool/credential use, perimeter crossing, external action, execution, or continuing turns.
