# Model-bound Profile evidence questioning and finding opening

Trust, Security, and Usability each consume one exact question authority against the sealed Profile and approved evidence chain. The evidentiary response is a deterministic, citation-bound projection of that record. No live witness, provider, assigned model, credential, tool, or external system is invoked.

Only after all three jurisdictional turns are sealed does the readiness artifact open one single-use Lord-Speaker finding-phase authority. Its separate consumption opens one independent finding authority per accepted Senator. It authors no finding and opens no deliberation or downstream operational authority.

The checkpoints are PROFILE_EXAMINATION_TESTIMONY_ANSWERS_SEALED_PENDING_FINDING_AUTHORITY_OPENING and PROFILE_EXAMINATION_FINDING_AUTHORITIES_OPENED_PENDING_SENATOR_FINDINGS.
